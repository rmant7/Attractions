Alicante

Alicante, a vibrant city located on the eastern coast of Spain, is a budget traveller's paradise. This city is a perfect blend of modernity and tradition, offering a unique experience to its visitors. The city is known for its rich culture, traditions, and stunning architecture that reflects its history. Alicante is also famous for its festivals, which are celebrated with great enthusiasm and fervour. The city's art scene is also thriving, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the works of local artists.

One of the most intriguing aspects of Alicante is its national food. The city is famous for its delicious paella, a rice dish that is cooked with seafood, chicken, or vegetables. The dish is a must-try for any food lover visiting the city. Alicante is also home to some of the most beautiful beaches in Spain, with crystal clear waters and golden sand. The city's natural beauty is breathtaking, with stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea.

Alicante is a city that surprises visitors with its unique features. The city's castle, Castillo de Santa Barbara, is perched on top of a hill and offers panoramic views of the city. The city's old town, Barrio de la Santa Cruz, is a maze of narrow streets and colourful houses that transport visitors back in time. The city's modern marina, Puerto de Alicante, is a hub of activity, with numerous restaurants, bars, and shops.

For budget travellers, Alicante offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions. The city's beaches are free to access, and visitors can enjoy a day of sun, sand, and sea without spending a dime. The city's museums and galleries also offer free entry on certain days of the week. The city's parks and gardens are also a great place to relax and unwind, with beautiful green spaces and stunning views.

In conclusion, Alicante is a city that offers something for everyone. Its rich culture, stunning architecture, delicious food, and natural beauty make it a must-visit destination for budget travellers. The city's unique features and free or low-cost attractions make it an ideal destination for those looking to explore Spain without breaking the bank.
