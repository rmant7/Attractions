Riga

Riga, the capital city of Latvia, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic European experience. This vibrant city boasts a rich history and culture, with stunning architecture that blends Gothic, Art Nouveau, and Baroque styles. The Old Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is a must-visit for its cobbled streets, charming cafes, and medieval buildings.

Riga is a city that celebrates its traditions and culture with pride. Visitors can immerse themselves in Latvian folklore and music at the Latvian Ethnographic Open-Air Museum, or witness the traditional Latvian dance and song festival, which takes place every five years. The city also hosts a range of festivals throughout the year, including the Riga Opera Festival and the Riga International Film Festival.

Art lovers will be delighted by Riga's thriving art scene, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing both contemporary and classical works. The Latvian National Museum of Art is a must-visit, with an impressive collection of Latvian and European art.

No visit to Riga is complete without indulging in the national cuisine. Latvian food is hearty and delicious, with dishes such as grey peas with bacon, potato pancakes, and smoked fish. The Central Market, housed in five former Zeppelin hangars, is a foodie's paradise, offering a wide range of local produce at affordable prices.

Riga is also full of surprises, with unique features that set it apart from other European cities. Did you know that Riga has the largest collection of Art Nouveau buildings in the world? Or that the city's skyline is dominated by the towering spires of St. Peter's Church and the Freedom Monument?

For budget travellers, Riga offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions. The city's parks and gardens are perfect for a leisurely stroll, while the Daugava River offers scenic boat tours. The Latvian War Museum and the Museum of the Occupation of Latvia are both free to enter, providing a fascinating insight into the country's history.

In summary, Riga is a city that offers something for everyone, from its rich culture and traditions to its stunning architecture and delicious cuisine. With affordable prices and plenty of free attractions, it's the perfect destination for budget travellers looking for an authentic European experience.
