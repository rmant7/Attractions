Montpellier

Montpellier is a vibrant city located in the south of France, known for its rich history, stunning architecture, and lively atmosphere. This city is a perfect destination for budget travellers who want to experience the best of French culture without breaking the bank. Montpellier is a city that is steeped in tradition, with a unique blend of old and new that is sure to captivate visitors.

The city is home to some of the most beautiful architecture in France, with stunning Gothic cathedrals, ancient Roman ruins, and elegant Renaissance buildings. Montpellier is also famous for its festivals, including the annual Montpellier Dance Festival, which attracts dancers from all over the world.

The city is also a hub for art and culture, with numerous museums and galleries showcasing the works of local and international artists. Visitors can also indulge in the local cuisine, which is a delicious blend of Mediterranean and French flavours. From fresh seafood to hearty stews, there is something for everyone to enjoy.

One of the most surprising facts about Montpellier is that it is home to the oldest medical school in Europe, dating back to the 12th century. Visitors can explore the historic campus and learn about the city's rich medical history.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Montpellier. The city's beautiful parks and gardens are perfect for a leisurely stroll, while the historic city centre is a great place to explore on foot. Visitors can also take advantage of the city's excellent public transport system to explore further afield.

In conclusion, Montpellier is a city that is sure to delight budget travellers with its rich history, stunning architecture, and lively atmosphere. Whether you're interested in art, culture, or cuisine, there is something for everyone to enjoy in this beautiful city. So why not plan your next adventure to Montpellier and experience the best of French culture for yourself?
