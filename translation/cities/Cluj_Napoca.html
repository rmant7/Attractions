Cluj-Napoca

Cluj-Napoca, the heart of Transylvania, is a city that will enchant budget travellers with its rich culture, traditions, and stunning architecture. This vibrant city is a melting pot of cultures, where the old and new blend seamlessly. Cluj-Napoca is a city that is steeped in history, with its medieval architecture and cobbled streets that will transport you back in time.

The city is home to a plethora of festivals and events that celebrate its unique culture and traditions. The Transylvania International Film Festival is a must-see for film enthusiasts, while the Electric Castle Festival is a music lover's paradise. The city's art scene is also thriving, with numerous galleries and exhibitions showcasing the works of local artists.

One of the highlights of Cluj-Napoca is its delicious national food. From hearty stews to sweet pastries, the city's cuisine is a fusion of Hungarian, Romanian, and German influences. The local street food is also a must-try, with vendors selling traditional dishes such as langos and chimney cake.

Cluj-Napoca is also home to some surprising attractions, such as the Turda Salt Mine, a stunning underground attraction that is free to visit. The city's botanical garden is also a hidden gem, with its beautiful flora and fauna providing a peaceful escape from the hustle and bustle of the city.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Cluj-Napoca. The city's central park is a great place to relax and enjoy the scenery, while the St. Michael's Church is a stunning example of Gothic architecture that is free to visit.

In conclusion, Cluj-Napoca is a city that will captivate budget travellers with its unique culture, traditions, and stunning architecture. With its delicious national food, vibrant art scene, and surprising attractions, this city is a must-visit for anyone looking for an unforgettable travel experience.
