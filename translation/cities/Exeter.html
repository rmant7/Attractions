Exeter

Exeter, a charming city located in the southwest of England, is a perfect destination for budget travellers seeking a mix of history, culture, and natural beauty. With its rich heritage dating back to the Roman era, Exeter boasts a unique blend of old-world charm and modern amenities. The city's stunning architecture, including the Gothic-style Exeter Cathedral and the medieval Guildhall, is a testament to its rich history.

Exeter is also known for its vibrant cultural scene, with numerous festivals and events taking place throughout the year. The Exeter Festival of South West Food and Drink, held annually in April, is a must-visit for foodies, while the Exeter Respect Festival celebrates diversity and promotes social justice.

Art lovers will appreciate the city's many galleries and museums, including the Royal Albert Memorial Museum and Art Gallery, which houses an impressive collection of art and artefacts from around the world. Exeter is also home to a thriving theatre scene, with performances ranging from classic plays to contemporary productions.

No visit to Exeter would be complete without sampling some of the city's delicious national food. From traditional Devon cream teas to locally sourced seafood, Exeter's culinary scene is sure to delight even the most discerning palate.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore, such as the beautiful Exeter Quayside, which offers stunning views of the River Exe and is home to a variety of independent shops and cafes. The city's many parks and gardens, including the stunning Rougemont Gardens, are also perfect for a leisurely stroll or picnic.

One of the most surprising facts about Exeter is that it was once the most westerly Roman fortified settlement in Britain. Visitors can explore the city's Roman history at the underground passages, a network of medieval tunnels that were once used to transport water to the city.

In contrast to its ancient history, Exeter is also a hub of innovation and technology, with a thriving business community and a growing reputation as a centre for digital industries.

Overall, Exeter is a city that offers something for everyone, from its rich history and culture to its stunning natural beauty and modern amenities. Whether you're a budget traveller or simply looking for a unique and exciting destination, Exeter is sure to leave a lasting impression.
