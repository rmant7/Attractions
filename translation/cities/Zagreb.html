Zagreb

Zagreb, the capital city of Croatia, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic European experience. This vibrant city is a melting pot of cultures, traditions, and architecture, making it a unique destination for those who want to explore the rich history of the Balkans. The city's medieval old town, with its cobblestone streets and colorful buildings, is a must-see for anyone interested in architecture.

Zagreb is also known for its lively festivals, such as the Zagreb Advent, which takes place during the Christmas season and features a variety of free events, including concerts, markets, and ice skating. The city is also home to numerous museums and galleries, showcasing the best of Croatian art and culture.

Foodies will love Zagreb's national cuisine, which is a fusion of Mediterranean and Central European flavors. From hearty stews to fresh seafood, there is something for everyone to enjoy. And for those on a budget, there are plenty of street food options and local markets to explore.

One of the most surprising facts about Zagreb is that it has a thriving street art scene, with colorful murals and graffiti adorning the city's walls. Visitors can take a free walking tour to discover some of the best street art in the city.

For those looking for free or low-cost attractions, Zagreb has plenty to offer. The city's parks and gardens are perfect for a picnic or a leisurely stroll, and the Zagreb Cathedral and St. Mark's Church are both free to enter.

In contrast to other European cities, Zagreb has a laid-back and relaxed atmosphere, making it the perfect destination for budget travellers looking to escape the crowds. With its unique blend of culture, history, and cuisine, Zagreb is a city that will leave a lasting impression on anyone who visits.
