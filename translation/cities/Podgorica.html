Podgorica

Podgorica, the capital city of Montenegro, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic Balkan experience. This vibrant city is a melting pot of cultures, with a rich history and a unique blend of architectural styles. From the Ottoman-era Clock Tower to the modern Millennium Bridge, Podgorica offers a fascinating contrast of old and new.

The city is also known for its lively festivals, such as the Podgorica Cultural Summer, which showcases local music, dance, and theatre. Visitors can immerse themselves in the local culture by trying traditional Montenegrin dishes, such as the hearty meat stew known as "karađorđeva šnicla" or the savory cheese pie called "burek".

Art lovers will appreciate the numerous galleries and museums, including the Contemporary Art Centre and the Natural History Museum. For those seeking outdoor activities, the nearby Lake Skadar National Park offers stunning scenery and hiking trails.

One surprising fact about Podgorica is that it is one of the few European capitals without a McDonald's restaurant. Instead, visitors can indulge in local street food, such as grilled meat skewers or "ćevapi".

Budget travellers will appreciate the many free or low-cost attractions in Podgorica, such as the Gorica Hill viewpoint, which offers panoramic views of the city, or the Ribnica Bridge, a historic stone bridge that spans the Morača River.

Overall, Podgorica is a city full of surprises and unique experiences, perfect for budget travellers looking to explore the lesser-known corners of Europe.
