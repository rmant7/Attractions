Florence

Florence, the capital of Tuscany, is a city that is steeped in history, culture, and art. It is a city that is perfect for budget travellers who want to experience the best of Italy without breaking the bank. The city is known for its stunning architecture, including the iconic Duomo, which dominates the skyline. The city is also home to some of the world's most famous art museums, including the Uffizi Gallery and the Accademia Gallery, which houses Michelangelo's David.

Florence is a city that is rich in culture and tradition. The city is famous for its festivals, including the Festa della Rificolona, which takes place in September and celebrates the birth of the Virgin Mary. The city is also known for its delicious food, including the famous Florentine steak, which is a must-try for any foodie.

One of the most surprising facts about Florence is that it was once the capital of Italy. The city is also home to the oldest pharmacy in the world, which has been in operation since 1221. Another interesting fact is that the Ponte Vecchio, one of the city's most famous landmarks, was the only bridge in Florence that was not destroyed during World War II.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Florence. The city's many parks and gardens are perfect for a picnic or a leisurely stroll. The Boboli Gardens, located behind the Pitti Palace, are a must-visit for anyone who loves nature. The city's many churches and cathedrals are also free to enter, including the Basilica di Santa Croce, which is the final resting place of many famous Italians, including Michelangelo and Galileo.

In conclusion, Florence is a city that is perfect for budget travellers who want to experience the best of Italy without breaking the bank. The city is rich in culture, tradition, and history, and there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy. Whether you're a foodie, an art lover, or a history buff, Florence has something for everyone.
