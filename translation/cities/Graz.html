Graz

Graz, the second-largest city in Austria, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic European experience. This charming city boasts a rich cultural heritage, with a blend of modern and traditional architecture that will leave you in awe. Graz is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and its historic centre is a testament to its rich history and cultural significance.

The city is known for its vibrant festivals, including the Graz International Jazz Festival and the Styrian Autumn Festival, which showcase the best of local and international music, art, and culture. Graz is also home to some of the most impressive museums and galleries in Austria, including the Kunsthaus Graz and the Landesmuseum Joanneum.

Graz is a food lover's paradise, with a wide range of local and international cuisine to choose from. The city is famous for its traditional Styrian cuisine, which includes dishes like pumpkin seed oil, Styrian apple strudel, and Styrian fried chicken. You can also find a variety of international restaurants and street food vendors that offer delicious and affordable meals.

One of the most surprising facts about Graz is that it has the highest number of students per capita in Austria, which gives the city a youthful and vibrant energy. Graz is also known for its green spaces, including the Schlossberg, a hill that offers stunning views of the city and is a popular spot for picnics and outdoor activities.

For budget travellers, Graz offers plenty of free and low-cost attractions, including the Graz Clock Tower, the Graz Cathedral, and the Eggenberg Palace. You can also explore the city's many parks and gardens, including the Botanical Garden and the Stadtpark.

In conclusion, Graz is a city that offers something for everyone, from its rich cultural heritage to its vibrant festivals, delicious cuisine, and stunning architecture. Whether you're a history buff, a foodie, or an art lover, Graz is a destination that will leave you with unforgettable memories.
