Seoul

Seoul, the bustling capital city of South Korea, is a vibrant destination that offers a unique blend of ancient traditions and modern innovations. This city is a perfect destination for budget travellers who want to experience the rich culture and history of Korea without breaking the bank. Seoul is a city that is steeped in history, with ancient palaces, temples, and shrines that are a testament to its rich cultural heritage.

The city is known for its stunning architecture, with towering skyscrapers and traditional hanok houses coexisting side by side. Seoul is also famous for its festivals, which celebrate everything from traditional Korean culture to modern art and music. The city's art scene is thriving, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the works of both local and international artists.

One of the highlights of any trip to Seoul is the food. Korean cuisine is famous for its bold flavors and unique ingredients, and Seoul is home to some of the best restaurants in the country. From street food to fine dining, there is something for every budget and taste.

Seoul is also a city of surprises, with hidden gems around every corner. Did you know that Seoul is home to the world's largest underground shopping mall? Or that the city has a vibrant street art scene that rivals that of any major city in the world?

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Seoul. From hiking in the city's many parks to visiting traditional markets and temples, there is no shortage of things to see and do. And with a world-class public transportation system, getting around the city is easy and affordable.

In short, Seoul is a city that has something for everyone. Whether you're interested in history, culture, art, or food, this vibrant destination is sure to leave a lasting impression. So why not book your trip today and experience the magic of Seoul for yourself?
