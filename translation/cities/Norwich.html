Norwich

Norwich, a charming city in the heart of East Anglia, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic English experience. With a rich history dating back to the Roman times, Norwich boasts a unique blend of medieval and modern architecture, making it a picturesque destination for sightseeing. The city is home to a vibrant arts scene, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing local talent and international exhibitions.

Norwich is also known for its lively festivals, including the famous Norfolk and Norwich Festival, which celebrates the city's cultural diversity through music, dance, and theatre performances. The city's culinary scene is equally impressive, with a range of traditional and contemporary restaurants serving up delicious local dishes, such as Norfolk dumplings and Cromer crab.

One of the most surprising facts about Norwich is that it has the highest concentration of medieval churches in the world, with over 30 still standing today. Visitors can explore these historic landmarks for free, including the stunning Norwich Cathedral and the ruins of St. Augustine's Church.

For those on a budget, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy, such as the beautiful Norwich Castle Gardens and the tranquil River Wensum. The city also offers a range of affordable accommodation options, from cosy guesthouses to budget-friendly hostels.

In contrast to the hustle and bustle of London, Norwich offers a more relaxed and laid-back atmosphere, making it the perfect destination for those seeking a peaceful getaway. With its unique blend of history, culture, and natural beauty, Norwich is a must-visit destination for budget travellers looking for an authentic English experience.
