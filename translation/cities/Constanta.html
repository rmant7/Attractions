Constanta

Constanta, the oldest city in Romania, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. This vibrant city is a melting pot of cultures, traditions, and architecture, with a rich history dating back to ancient times. The city's stunning landmarks, such as the Roman mosaic and the Great Mosque, are a testament to its diverse past.

The city is also home to a plethora of festivals and events, including the Constanta International Film Festival and the Constanta Jazz Festival, which showcase the city's vibrant arts scene. The local cuisine is a fusion of Romanian, Turkish, and Greek influences, with dishes such as sarmale and mici being must-tries for foodies.

One of the most surprising facts about Constanta is that it is home to the largest port on the Black Sea, making it a hub for international trade and commerce. The city's bustling markets and bazaars are a testament to this, offering a unique shopping experience for visitors.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore, such as the beautiful beaches of Mamaia and the stunning Danube Delta. The city's museums and galleries, such as the National History and Archeology Museum and the Art Museum, offer a glimpse into the city's rich cultural heritage.

In contrast to the hustle and bustle of the city, Constanta also offers a peaceful retreat in the form of the Carol I Park, a beautiful green space with stunning views of the Black Sea. The park is the perfect place to relax and unwind after a day of exploring the city.

Overall, Constanta is a city that offers something for everyone, from its rich cultural heritage to its stunning architecture and beautiful beaches. It is a must-visit destination for budget travellers looking for an authentic cultural experience.
