Bucharest

Bucharest, the capital city of Romania, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic European experience. This vibrant city is a melting pot of cultures, traditions, and architecture, with a rich history dating back to the 15th century. Bucharest is a city of contrasts, where the old and new blend seamlessly, creating a unique atmosphere that is both charming and exciting.

The city is home to a plethora of festivals and events throughout the year, including the Bucharest International Film Festival, the George Enescu Festival, and the Bucharest Jazz Festival. These events showcase the city's love for art and culture, and provide visitors with a chance to experience the local traditions and customs.

Bucharest's architecture is a mix of styles, from the grandiose buildings of the Belle Époque era to the modern skyscrapers that dominate the skyline. The city's most famous landmark is the Palace of the Parliament, the second-largest administrative building in the world, which is a must-see for any visitor.

The city's food scene is a fusion of traditional Romanian cuisine and international influences, with plenty of options for budget travellers. From hearty stews and soups to delicious pastries and street food, Bucharest's culinary offerings are sure to delight your taste buds.

For those on a budget, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore, such as the beautiful parks and gardens, the National Museum of Art, and the Old Town, which is a maze of narrow streets and historic buildings.

Bucharest is a city that surprises and intrigues visitors with its unique blend of history, culture, and modernity. It's a city that appeals to the senses and emotions, with its vibrant energy and welcoming atmosphere. So, pack your bags and head to Bucharest for an unforgettable budget-friendly adventure.
