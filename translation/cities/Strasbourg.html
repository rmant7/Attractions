Strasbourg

Strasbourg, the capital of the Alsace region in northeastern France, is a charming city that offers a unique blend of French and German cultures. With its picturesque canals, half-timbered houses, and stunning Gothic cathedral, Strasbourg is a must-visit destination for budget travellers looking for an authentic European experience.

The city is steeped in history and tradition, with a rich cultural heritage that is reflected in its architecture, festivals, and art. The Strasbourg Cathedral, a masterpiece of Gothic architecture, is a sight to behold, with its intricate carvings and stunning stained glass windows. The city is also home to numerous museums and galleries, showcasing everything from contemporary art to ancient artefacts.

One of the most exciting things about Strasbourg is its vibrant festival scene. The city hosts a number of festivals throughout the year, including the famous Christmas market, which attracts visitors from all over the world. The market is a feast for the senses, with its delicious food, mulled wine, and festive atmosphere.

When it comes to food, Strasbourg is famous for its Alsatian cuisine, which is a delicious blend of French and German flavours. From hearty stews and sausages to delicate pastries and tarts, there is something for everyone to enjoy. And with plenty of affordable restaurants and cafes, budget travellers can indulge in the local cuisine without breaking the bank.

For those looking for free or low-cost attractions, Strasbourg has plenty to offer. The city's historic centre is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and is home to numerous landmarks and monuments that can be explored on foot. The Petite France neighbourhood, with its charming canals and colourful houses, is a particularly popular spot for visitors.

Overall, Strasbourg is a city that is sure to captivate and enchant budget travellers. With its unique blend of cultures, stunning architecture, and vibrant festivals, it is a destination that should not be missed.
