Riyadh

Riyadh, the capital city of Saudi Arabia, is a fascinating destination for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. The city is steeped in rich history and traditions, with stunning architecture that reflects its Islamic heritage. The vibrant festivals and art scene add to the city's charm, making it a must-visit destination for art enthusiasts.

The city's national food is a delight for foodies, with its unique blend of spices and flavors that tantalize the taste buds. The local cuisine is a fusion of Middle Eastern and Asian influences, with dishes like Kabsa, a rice-based dish, and Shawarma, a popular street food.

Riyadh is also home to some surprising facts that will intrigue visitors. For instance, the city is home to the world's largest camel market, where visitors can witness the trading of these majestic animals. The city also boasts the world's largest indoor theme park, making it a perfect destination for families.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Riyadh. The city's museums and galleries offer a glimpse into the country's rich history and culture, while the bustling souks and markets are perfect for bargain hunters.

The city's unique features are highlighted by its stark contrasts, with modern skyscrapers standing alongside ancient fortresses and palaces. The city's desert landscape adds to its allure, with sand dunes and rocky outcrops providing a stunning backdrop to the city's architecture.

In conclusion, Riyadh is a city that offers a unique blend of culture, history, and modernity, making it an ideal destination for budget travellers seeking an authentic experience. With its stunning architecture, vibrant festivals, and delicious cuisine, Riyadh is a city that will leave visitors with lasting memories.
