Fuerteventura

Fuerteventura is a stunning island located in the Canary Islands, Spain, that is perfect for budget travellers seeking a unique and unforgettable experience. This island is known for its breathtaking beaches, crystal-clear waters, and year-round sunshine, making it an ideal destination for those who love to soak up the sun and enjoy water activities such as surfing, windsurfing, and kitesurfing.

Aside from its natural beauty, Fuerteventura is also rich in culture and traditions. The island's architecture is a blend of Spanish and African influences, with white-washed buildings and colorful doors and windows. The locals are friendly and welcoming, and visitors can immerse themselves in the island's culture by attending traditional festivals and events such as the Carnival of Fuerteventura, which takes place in February.

Art lovers will appreciate the island's vibrant art scene, with many galleries and exhibitions showcasing the work of local artists. Foodies will also be delighted by the island's national food, which includes fresh seafood, goat cheese, and papas arrugadas (wrinkled potatoes) served with mojo sauce.

One of the most surprising facts about Fuerteventura is that it is home to the largest sand dunes in the Canary Islands, located in the Corralejo Natural Park. Visitors can explore the dunes on foot or by camel, and enjoy stunning views of the Atlantic Ocean.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy on the island. These include hiking in the Betancuria Rural Park, visiting the Tefia Craft Village, and exploring the island's many museums and cultural centers.

In conclusion, Fuerteventura is a must-visit destination for budget travellers seeking a unique and unforgettable experience. With its stunning natural beauty, rich culture and traditions, vibrant art scene, delicious national food, and surprising facts, this island is sure to captivate the senses and leave a lasting impression.
