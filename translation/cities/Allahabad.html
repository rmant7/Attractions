Allahabad

Allahabad, also known as Prayagraj, is a city in the northern Indian state of Uttar Pradesh. It is a city that is steeped in history and culture, making it a must-visit destination for budget travellers. The city is known for its beautiful architecture, which is a blend of Mughal and British styles. The city is also home to some of the most important Hindu temples in India, including the famous Kumbh Mela.

Allahabad is a city that is rich in tradition and culture. The city is known for its vibrant festivals, including the Kumbh Mela, which is the largest religious gathering in the world. The city is also home to a number of museums and art galleries, which showcase the rich history and culture of the region.

One of the most unique features of Allahabad is its cuisine. The city is known for its delicious street food, which is a blend of Indian and Mughlai flavours. Some of the must-try dishes include chaat, samosas, and kebabs.

Allahabad is also home to some of the most beautiful parks and gardens in India. The city's parks are perfect for a relaxing afternoon stroll or a picnic with friends and family. Some of the most popular parks include the Allahabad Fort, Anand Bhawan, and the Khusro Bagh.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions in Allahabad. The city's museums and art galleries are often free to enter, and there are plenty of parks and gardens that are open to the public. The city is also home to a number of beautiful temples, which are free to visit.

In conclusion, Allahabad is a city that is rich in history, culture, and tradition. It is a must-visit destination for budget travellers who are looking to experience the best of India's culture and heritage. With its beautiful architecture, vibrant festivals, delicious cuisine, and stunning parks and gardens, Allahabad is a city that is sure to leave a lasting impression on all who visit.
