Helsinki

Helsinki, the capital city of Finland, is a vibrant and exciting destination for budget travellers. This city is a perfect blend of modernity and tradition, with a rich cultural heritage that is sure to captivate visitors. Helsinki is known for its stunning architecture, with a mix of neoclassical and modern buildings that create a unique skyline. The city is also home to a thriving arts scene, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the best of Finnish art.

One of the most surprising things about Helsinki is its love for saunas. The city has over 3 million saunas, which is more than the entire population of Finland! Visitors can experience this Finnish tradition by visiting one of the many public saunas in the city. Another unique feature of Helsinki is its proximity to nature. The city is surrounded by beautiful forests and lakes, making it a great destination for outdoor enthusiasts.

Helsinki is also known for its delicious food, with traditional Finnish dishes like reindeer stew and smoked salmon being popular choices. Visitors can sample these dishes at one of the many affordable restaurants in the city. Helsinki is also home to numerous festivals throughout the year, including the Helsinki Festival and the Helsinki Design Week.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Helsinki. The city has numerous parks and gardens, including the beautiful Esplanade Park. Visitors can also explore the historic Suomenlinna Fortress, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The Helsinki Cathedral is another must-see attraction, with its stunning white facade and impressive dome.

In conclusion, Helsinki is a city that has something for everyone. From its rich cultural heritage to its stunning architecture and delicious food, this city is sure to captivate visitors. With plenty of free or low-cost attractions, it's also a great destination for budget travellers. So why not plan your next trip to Helsinki and experience all that this amazing city has to offer?
