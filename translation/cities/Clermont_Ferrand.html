Clermont-Ferrand

Clermont-Ferrand, a charming city nestled in the heart of France, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic French experience. The city boasts a rich cultural heritage, with a unique blend of Roman and Gothic architecture that will leave you in awe. The iconic black volcanic stone used in many of the buildings gives the city a distinctive look and feel.

The city is steeped in tradition, with many festivals and events throughout the year. The Festival du Court-Métrage, a short film festival, is a must-see for film enthusiasts. The Fête de la Musique, a music festival held every June, is a celebration of all genres of music and is free to attend.

Art lovers will be delighted by the Musée d'Art Roger-Quilliot, which houses an impressive collection of contemporary art. Foodies will be in heaven with the local cuisine, which includes the famous Auvergne cheese and charcuterie. The city is also home to many affordable restaurants and cafes, where you can indulge in delicious French cuisine without breaking the bank.

One of the most surprising facts about Clermont-Ferrand is that it is home to the Michelin tire company, which has its headquarters in the city. Visitors can learn about the history of the company at the Michelin Adventure Museum, which is free to enter.

For those on a budget, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy. The Jardin Lecoq, a beautiful park in the city center, is a great place to relax and take in the scenery. The Cathédrale Notre-Dame-de-l'Assomption, a stunning Gothic cathedral, is also free to visit.

In contrast to the bustling cities of Paris and Lyon, Clermont-Ferrand offers a more laid-back and authentic French experience. The city's unique blend of culture, tradition, and architecture make it a must-visit destination for budget travellers looking for an off-the-beaten-path adventure.
