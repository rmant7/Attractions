Rome

Rome, the eternal city, is a destination that budget travellers cannot miss. This city is a treasure trove of culture, traditions, and architecture that dates back to ancient times. The city is home to some of the world's most iconic landmarks, such as the Colosseum, the Pantheon, and the Roman Forum. The city's rich history is evident in its stunning architecture, which is a blend of ancient, medieval, and modern styles.

Rome is also famous for its festivals, such as the Carnival of Rome, which takes place in February, and the Festa della Repubblica, which is celebrated on June 2nd. These festivals are a great way to experience the city's vibrant culture and traditions.

Art lovers will be delighted to know that Rome is home to some of the world's most famous art museums, such as the Vatican Museums and the Galleria Borghese. These museums house some of the most beautiful and priceless works of art, including paintings, sculptures, and tapestries.

When it comes to food, Rome is a foodie's paradise. The city is famous for its delicious pasta dishes, such as carbonara and cacio e pepe, as well as its mouth-watering pizza. Visitors can also indulge in some of the city's traditional dishes, such as supplì, a deep-fried rice ball filled with mozzarella cheese.

One of the most surprising facts about Rome is that it has more than 900 churches, each with its own unique history and architecture. Visitors can explore these churches for free and marvel at their stunning beauty.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions in Rome, such as the Trevi Fountain, the Spanish Steps, and the Piazza Navona. These attractions are a great way to experience the city's beauty without breaking the bank.

In conclusion, Rome is a city that offers something for everyone, from its rich history and culture to its delicious food and stunning architecture. It is a city that will leave visitors in awe and wanting more.
