Amman

Amman, the capital city of Jordan, is a vibrant and bustling metropolis that offers a unique blend of ancient history and modernity. This city is a perfect destination for budget travellers who want to experience the rich culture and traditions of the Middle East without breaking the bank. The city's architecture is a mix of old and new, with ancient ruins and modern skyscrapers coexisting side by side.

Amman is a city that is steeped in history and culture, with a rich heritage that dates back thousands of years. The city is home to numerous museums and galleries that showcase the country's art and history, including the Jordan Museum and the Royal Automobile Museum. The city is also famous for its delicious national food, including dishes like mansaf, falafel, and hummus.

One of the most surprising facts about Amman is that it is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world, with a history that dates back over 9,000 years. The city is also known for its vibrant festivals and celebrations, including the Jerash Festival of Culture and Arts and the Amman International Film Festival.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Amman. The city's ancient ruins, including the Roman Amphitheatre and the Citadel, are must-see attractions that won't cost you a dime. The city's bustling markets, including the famous Rainbow Street, are also great places to explore and soak up the local culture.

In contrast to the city's ancient history, Amman is also a modern and cosmopolitan city, with a thriving arts and culture scene. The city is home to numerous galleries and art spaces, including the Darat al Funun and the Jordan National Gallery of Fine Arts.

Overall, Amman is a city that offers something for everyone, from ancient history and culture to modern art and cuisine. Whether you're a budget traveller or a luxury seeker, this city is sure to captivate your senses and leave you with unforgettable memories.
