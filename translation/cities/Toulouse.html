Toulouse

Toulouse, the Pink City, is a vibrant and charming destination that offers budget travellers a wealth of experiences. This city is steeped in history and culture, with a rich heritage that dates back to the Roman era. Its stunning architecture, from the Gothic Cathédrale Saint-Étienne to the Renaissance Hôtel d'Assézat, is a testament to its past.

Toulouse is also known for its lively festivals, such as the Fête de la Musique and the Festival Occitania, which celebrate the city's unique culture and traditions. The city is home to a thriving arts scene, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the works of local artists.

One of the most intriguing aspects of Toulouse is its cuisine. The city is famous for its cassoulet, a hearty stew made with white beans, sausage, and duck or pork. Visitors can also sample other local delicacies, such as foie gras and confit de canard.

For budget travellers, Toulouse offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions. The Jardin des Plantes, a beautiful botanical garden, is a must-visit, as is the Musée des Augustins, which houses an impressive collection of medieval and Renaissance art. The Canal du Midi, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is another popular destination for visitors.

Toulouse is a city that appeals to the senses, with its vibrant colours, delicious smells, and lively sounds. Its unique blend of old-world charm and modern energy makes it a truly unforgettable destination. So why not pack your bags and discover the Pink City for yourself?
