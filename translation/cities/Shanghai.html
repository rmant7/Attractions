Shanghai

Shanghai, the bustling metropolis of China, is a city that never sleeps. It is a perfect destination for budget travellers who want to experience the rich culture and traditions of China without breaking the bank. The city is a perfect blend of modernity and tradition, with towering skyscrapers and ancient temples coexisting in harmony.

Shanghai is known for its unique architecture, with the Oriental Pearl Tower and the Shanghai Tower being the most iconic landmarks. The city is also famous for its festivals, including the Shanghai International Film Festival and the Shanghai International Arts Festival. These festivals showcase the best of Chinese art and culture and are a must-visit for any budget traveller.

The city is also a food lover's paradise, with a wide range of national dishes to choose from. From the famous xiaolongbao to the mouth-watering shengjianbao, Shanghai's street food scene is a must-try for any foodie.

Shanghai is also home to some of the most beautiful art galleries and museums in China. The Shanghai Museum and the China Art Museum are two of the most popular attractions for budget travellers. These museums showcase the rich history and culture of China and are a great way to learn more about the country's past.

One of the most surprising facts about Shanghai is that it has the world's fastest train, the Maglev, which can reach speeds of up to 430 km/h. Another interesting fact is that the city has the world's largest Starbucks, which is a must-visit for any coffee lover.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions in Shanghai. The Bund, a waterfront promenade, offers stunning views of the city skyline and is completely free to visit. The Yuyuan Garden, a traditional Chinese garden, is also a must-visit and costs only a few yuan to enter.

In conclusion, Shanghai is a city that offers something for everyone. Its unique culture, traditions, architecture, festivals, art, and national food make it a must-visit destination for budget travellers. With plenty of free or low-cost attractions, there's no reason not to add Shanghai to your travel bucket list.
