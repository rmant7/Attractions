Prague

Prague, the capital city of the Czech Republic, is a budget traveller's dream destination. This city is a perfect blend of history, culture, and modernity. The city is known for its stunning architecture, which ranges from Gothic to Baroque to Art Nouveau. The city's Old Town Square is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and is home to the famous Astronomical Clock, which dates back to the 15th century.

Prague is a city that is steeped in history and tradition. The city has a rich cultural heritage, and visitors can explore its many museums, galleries, and theatres. The city is also known for its vibrant festivals, including the Prague Spring International Music Festival and the Prague Fringe Festival.

The city's cuisine is a mix of traditional Czech dishes and international cuisine. Visitors can sample local delicacies such as goulash, roast pork with dumplings, and traditional Czech beer. The city is also home to a thriving arts scene, with many galleries and museums showcasing the works of local artists.

Prague is a city that is full of surprises. Visitors can explore the city's many hidden courtyards and alleyways, which are home to some of the city's best cafes and restaurants. The city is also home to the largest castle complex in the world, the Prague Castle, which dates back to the 9th century.

For budget travellers, Prague offers many free or low-cost attractions. Visitors can explore the city's many parks and gardens, including the beautiful Petrin Hill. The city is also home to many free museums and galleries, including the National Museum and the Museum of Decorative Arts.

In conclusion, Prague is a city that is full of history, culture, and surprises. It is a perfect destination for budget travellers who want to explore a city that is rich in tradition and heritage. With its stunning architecture, vibrant festivals, and delicious cuisine, Prague is a city that will leave visitors wanting more.
