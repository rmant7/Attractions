Vaxjo

Vaxjo, a charming city in southern Sweden, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic Scandinavian experience. This vibrant city is known for its rich culture, traditions, and stunning architecture that dates back to the 19th century. The city is also home to several festivals and events that showcase the best of Swedish culture, including the popular Vaxjo Art Biennial.

One of the most intriguing aspects of Vaxjo is its commitment to sustainability. The city has been named the greenest city in Europe, with a focus on renewable energy, eco-friendly transportation, and sustainable living. Visitors can explore the city's green spaces, including the beautiful Vaxjo Lake, which offers stunning views and a peaceful atmosphere.

Vaxjo is also a hub for art and culture, with several museums and galleries showcasing the works of local and international artists. The city's food scene is also a must-try, with traditional Swedish dishes like meatballs, herring, and lingonberry jam. For those on a budget, there are plenty of low-cost options, including food trucks and street vendors.

One of the most surprising facts about Vaxjo is its connection to the famous author Astrid Lindgren, who was born in the nearby town of Vimmerby. Visitors can explore the Astrid Lindgren's World theme park, which brings to life the beloved characters from her books.

For those looking for free or low-cost attractions, Vaxjo has plenty to offer. The city's beautiful parks and gardens are perfect for a picnic or a leisurely stroll, while the Vaxjo Cathedral is a stunning example of Gothic architecture. Visitors can also explore the city's many bike paths and hiking trails, which offer breathtaking views of the surrounding countryside.

In conclusion, Vaxjo is a must-visit destination for budget travellers looking for an authentic Swedish experience. With its rich culture, stunning architecture, and commitment to sustainability, this vibrant city offers something for everyone. So pack your bags and get ready to explore the hidden gem of southern Sweden!
