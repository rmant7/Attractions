Thiruvananthapuram

Thiruvananthapuram, also known as Trivandrum, is a vibrant city located in the southern state of Kerala, India. This city is a perfect destination for budget travellers who want to experience the rich culture and traditions of India without breaking the bank. Thiruvananthapuram is a city that is steeped in history and boasts of a unique blend of colonial and traditional architecture.

The city is famous for its festivals, including Onam, which is celebrated with great pomp and show. The city is also home to some of the most beautiful temples in India, including the Padmanabhaswamy Temple, which is a must-visit for anyone interested in Indian architecture and culture.

Thiruvananthapuram is also known for its delicious cuisine, which is a blend of traditional Kerala food and international cuisine. The city is famous for its seafood, and you can find some of the best seafood restaurants in the city.

One of the most surprising facts about Thiruvananthapuram is that it is home to one of the richest temples in the world, the Padmanabhaswamy Temple. The temple is said to have treasures worth billions of dollars, making it one of the most intriguing places to visit in the city.

For budget travellers, Thiruvananthapuram offers a range of free or low-cost attractions, including the Napier Museum, which houses a collection of ancient artefacts and art. The city is also home to some of the most beautiful beaches in India, including Kovalam Beach, which is a great place to relax and soak up the sun.

In conclusion, Thiruvananthapuram is a city that offers a unique blend of culture, history, and tradition. It is a must-visit destination for anyone interested in Indian architecture, festivals, and cuisine. With its range of free or low-cost attractions, Thiruvananthapuram is the perfect destination for budget travellers looking to experience the best of India.
