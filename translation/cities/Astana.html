Astana

Astana, the capital city of Kazakhstan, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. This modern city boasts a unique blend of traditional Kazakh culture and contemporary architecture, making it a fascinating destination for those seeking something different. The city's skyline is dominated by futuristic skyscrapers, such as the Bayterek Tower, which offers stunning views of the city.

Despite its modernity, Astana has a rich history and culture that is evident in its many museums and galleries. The National Museum of Kazakhstan is a must-visit for those interested in the country's history, while the Palace of Peace and Reconciliation is a stunning example of modern architecture that houses a variety of cultural exhibitions.

Astana is also known for its vibrant festivals and events, such as the Astana Day celebration in July and the Eurasia International Film Festival in September. These events offer a unique opportunity to experience the city's culture and traditions firsthand.

Foodies will love Astana's national cuisine, which features hearty meat dishes, such as beshbarmak and kazy, as well as a variety of dairy products, such as kurt and ayran. Visitors can sample these dishes at local restaurants and markets, which offer affordable options for budget travellers.

For those on a tight budget, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Astana. The city's parks, such as the Central Park of Culture and Recreation, offer a peaceful escape from the hustle and bustle of the city, while the Khan Shatyr Entertainment Center offers a unique shopping experience under a giant transparent tent.

Overall, Astana is a city that surprises and intrigues visitors with its unique blend of modernity and tradition. Its rich culture, stunning architecture, and delicious cuisine make it a must-visit destination for budget travellers seeking an authentic experience.
