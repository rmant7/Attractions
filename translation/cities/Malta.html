Malta

Malta, a small island nation in the Mediterranean, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking a unique cultural experience. With a rich history dating back to 4000 BC, Malta boasts a fascinating blend of influences from the Phoenicians, Romans, Arabs, and British. The architecture is a stunning mix of Baroque, Gothic, and Renaissance styles, with the capital city of Valletta being a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Malta is also known for its vibrant festivals, such as the Carnival in February and the Feast of St. Paul's Shipwreck in February/March. The island's art scene is thriving, with numerous galleries and exhibitions showcasing local talent. And let's not forget about the food - Maltese cuisine is a delicious fusion of Mediterranean and North African flavors, with dishes like rabbit stew and pastizzi (flaky pastries filled with ricotta or peas) being must-tries.

One surprising fact about Malta is that it has some of the oldest free-standing structures in the world, including the Megalithic Temples of Malta, which date back to 3600 BC. Another unique feature of Malta is its stunning blue waters, perfect for swimming, snorkeling, and diving.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy, such as exploring the charming streets of Valletta, visiting the Upper Barrakka Gardens for panoramic views of the harbor, or taking a dip at the picturesque Blue Lagoon. And with affordable accommodation options and a reliable public transportation system, Malta is a budget-friendly destination that won't disappoint.

In contrast to other Mediterranean destinations, Malta offers a unique blend of history, culture, and natural beauty that is sure to captivate any traveller. So why not add this hidden gem to your travel bucket list and experience the magic of Malta for yourself?
