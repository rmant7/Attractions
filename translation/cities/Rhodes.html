Rhodes

Rhodes, the largest of the Dodecanese islands in Greece, is a budget traveller's paradise. This island is a perfect blend of ancient history, stunning beaches, and vibrant culture. The city of Rhodes is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and it's easy to see why. The medieval old town is a maze of narrow streets, ancient walls, and impressive architecture that will transport you back in time.

The island's culture and traditions are deeply rooted in its history, and you can experience this through its festivals and art. The annual Medieval Rose Festival is a must-see, where the city comes alive with music, dance, and theatre performances. The island is also home to many museums and galleries, showcasing the rich history and art of Rhodes.

The national food of Rhodes is a delicious blend of Greek and Turkish cuisine, with a focus on fresh seafood, grilled meats, and local vegetables. You can find many affordable restaurants and street food vendors serving up these delicious dishes.

Rhodes is also home to some surprising natural wonders, such as the Valley of the Butterflies, where thousands of butterflies gather each year. The island is also known for its stunning beaches, with crystal clear waters and soft sand.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy. You can explore the old town on foot, visit the ancient Acropolis of Rhodes, or take a hike in the beautiful countryside. The island is also home to many affordable accommodations, from hostels to guesthouses.

In conclusion, Rhodes is a budget traveller's dream destination, offering a unique blend of history, culture, and natural beauty. Whether you're interested in exploring ancient ruins, relaxing on the beach, or experiencing local cuisine, Rhodes has something for everyone. So pack your bags and get ready to discover this hidden gem in the Mediterranean.
