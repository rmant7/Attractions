Belgrade

Belgrade, the capital city of Serbia, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic European experience. This vibrant city is a melting pot of cultures, traditions, and architecture, with a rich history that dates back to the Roman Empire. Belgrade is a city that never sleeps, with a lively nightlife scene that attracts visitors from all over the world.

The city's architecture is a mix of styles, from the Ottoman Empire to the Austro-Hungarian Empire, with a touch of modernity. The Kalemegdan Fortress, located at the confluence of the Sava and Danube rivers, is a must-see attraction that offers stunning views of the city. Belgrade is also home to numerous museums and galleries, showcasing the city's art and history.

Belgrade is a city of festivals, with events taking place throughout the year. The Belgrade Beer Fest, held in August, is the largest beer festival in Southeast Europe, attracting over half a million visitors. The Belgrade International Film Festival, held in February, is another popular event that showcases the best of Serbian and international cinema.

Serbian cuisine is a highlight of any visit to Belgrade, with hearty dishes that are sure to satisfy any appetite. The national dish, cevapi, is a grilled meat dish served with onions and bread. Other popular dishes include goulash, sarma, and pljeskavica. Belgrade is also known for its coffee culture, with numerous cafes serving up strong, delicious coffee.

Belgrade is full of surprises, with hidden gems around every corner. The city's underground tunnels, used during World War II, are now open to the public for tours. The Ada Ciganlija lake, located just outside the city, is a popular spot for swimming, sunbathing, and water sports.

For budget travellers, Belgrade offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions. The Nikola Tesla Museum, dedicated to the famous inventor, is a must-see attraction that won't break the bank. The Skadarlija district, known as the bohemian quarter, is a charming neighbourhood filled with cafes, restaurants, and street performers.

In conclusion, Belgrade is a city that offers something for everyone, from its rich history and culture to its vibrant nightlife and delicious cuisine. With its affordable prices and welcoming locals, Belgrade is a destination that should be on every budget traveller's list.
