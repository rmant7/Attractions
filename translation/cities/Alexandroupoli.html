Alexandroupoli

Alexandroupoli, a charming city located in the northeastern part of Greece, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic Greek experience. This city is a melting pot of cultures, with a rich history and a unique blend of traditions that will leave you in awe. The architecture of Alexandroupoli is a mix of Ottoman and European styles, creating a unique and fascinating atmosphere that will transport you back in time.

One of the most exciting things about Alexandroupoli is its festivals. The city hosts a variety of cultural events throughout the year, including the famous Carnival of Alexandroupoli, which is a must-see for anyone visiting the city. The art scene in Alexandroupoli is also thriving, with numerous galleries and exhibitions showcasing the work of local artists.

When it comes to food, Alexandroupoli has a lot to offer. The city is known for its delicious seafood, and you can find a variety of traditional Greek dishes at affordable prices. Don't miss out on trying the local specialty, "soutzoukakia," which are meatballs in a tomato sauce.

One of the most surprising things about Alexandroupoli is its natural beauty. The city is surrounded by stunning beaches and lush green forests, making it the perfect destination for nature lovers. Some of the free or low-cost attractions include the Evros Delta National Park, the Alexandroupoli Lighthouse, and the Ethnological Museum.

In contrast to the bustling cities of Athens and Thessaloniki, Alexandroupoli offers a more laid-back and relaxed atmosphere. The locals are friendly and welcoming, and you'll feel right at home in this charming city. Whether you're looking to explore the rich history and culture of Greece or simply relax on the beach, Alexandroupoli has something for everyone.
