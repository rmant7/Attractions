Dhaka

Dhaka, the bustling capital city of Bangladesh, is a vibrant and exciting destination for budget travellers. With a rich history and culture, Dhaka offers a unique blend of old and new, traditional and modern. The city is home to stunning architecture, including the iconic Ahsan Manzil palace and the historic Lalbagh Fort. Visitors can also explore the vibrant street markets and bazaars, where they can find everything from traditional handicrafts to delicious street food.

Dhaka is a city of festivals, with a calendar full of colorful celebrations throughout the year. From the vibrant Bengali New Year celebrations to the religious festivals of Eid and Durga Puja, there is always something to see and experience in Dhaka. The city is also home to a thriving arts scene, with galleries and museums showcasing the work of local artists and craftsmen.

One of the highlights of any visit to Dhaka is the opportunity to sample the delicious national cuisine. From spicy curries to sweet desserts, the food in Dhaka is a feast for the senses. Visitors can try traditional dishes like biryani, dal, and chutney, or sample street food like samosas and jhal muri.

Despite its reputation as a busy and crowded city, Dhaka has plenty of free or low-cost attractions for budget travellers. Visitors can explore the tranquil beauty of the Ramna Park, or take a boat ride on the Buriganga River. The city is also home to a number of historic mosques and temples, including the stunning Star Mosque and the colorful Dhakeshwari Temple.

For those looking for a unique and exciting travel experience, Dhaka is a must-visit destination. With its rich culture, vibrant festivals, and delicious food, this city is sure to leave a lasting impression on any visitor.
