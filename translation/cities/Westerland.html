Westerland

Westerland, the largest town on the German island of Sylt, is a budget traveller's paradise. This charming coastal town is known for its stunning beaches, vibrant culture, and rich history. The town's architecture is a mix of traditional and modern styles, with colorful buildings lining the streets. Visitors can explore the town's many museums and galleries, which showcase the island's unique culture and traditions.

One of the most exciting events in Westerland is the annual Sylt Surf Cup, which attracts surfers from around the world. The town is also home to a number of art festivals and exhibitions, showcasing the work of local artists. Foodies will love the town's delicious seafood, which is caught fresh daily and served in many of the local restaurants.

Westerland is also home to a number of free or low-cost attractions, including the stunning Westerland Beach, which offers miles of pristine sand and crystal-clear water. Visitors can also explore the town's many parks and gardens, which are perfect for a relaxing afternoon stroll.

One of the most surprising facts about Westerland is that it is home to the largest concentration of thatched-roof houses in Germany. These charming homes are a testament to the town's rich history and traditional way of life.

Overall, Westerland is a must-visit destination for budget travellers looking for a unique and exciting experience. With its stunning beaches, vibrant culture, and rich history, this charming coastal town is sure to leave a lasting impression on visitors.
