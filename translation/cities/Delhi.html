Delhi

Delhi, the capital city of India, is a vibrant and bustling metropolis that offers a unique blend of ancient and modern cultures. With its rich history, diverse traditions, and stunning architecture, Delhi is a must-visit destination for budget travellers looking to explore the heart of India.

The city is home to some of the most iconic landmarks in the country, including the Red Fort, Qutub Minar, and India Gate. These architectural marvels are a testament to the city's rich cultural heritage and offer a glimpse into its glorious past.

Delhi is also known for its vibrant festivals and celebrations, such as Diwali, Holi, and Eid. These festivals are a great way to experience the city's unique culture and traditions, and are sure to leave a lasting impression on visitors.

The city is also a food lover's paradise, with a wide range of delicious and affordable street food options available. From spicy chaat to mouth-watering parathas, Delhi's street food scene is a must-try for anyone visiting the city.

In addition to its cultural and culinary offerings, Delhi also boasts a thriving art scene, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the works of local and international artists.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Delhi, such as the Lotus Temple, Humayun's Tomb, and the National Museum. These attractions offer a great way to experience the city's unique culture and history without breaking the bank.

Overall, Delhi is a city that is sure to captivate and inspire visitors with its rich culture, stunning architecture, and vibrant energy. Whether you're a history buff, foodie, or art lover, there's something for everyone in this incredible city.
