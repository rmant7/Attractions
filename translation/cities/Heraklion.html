Heraklion

Heraklion, the capital city of Crete, is a budget traveller's paradise. This vibrant city is steeped in history and culture, with a rich tapestry of traditions, architecture, festivals, art, and national food. The city's ancient Venetian walls and fortress, combined with its bustling markets and lively tavernas, create a unique atmosphere that is both charming and exciting.

Heraklion is home to some of the most impressive archaeological sites in Greece, including the Palace of Knossos, which dates back to the Minoan civilization. Visitors can also explore the city's many museums, which showcase the island's rich history and culture.

One of the most exciting aspects of Heraklion is its vibrant festival scene. The city hosts a number of festivals throughout the year, including the Heraklion Summer Arts Festival, which features music, dance, and theatre performances from around the world.

Foodies will love Heraklion's delicious cuisine, which is a fusion of Greek, Mediterranean, and Middle Eastern flavors. From fresh seafood to traditional Cretan dishes like dakos and kalitsounia, there is something for everyone to enjoy.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Heraklion. The city's beautiful beaches, like Ammoudara and Kokkini Hani, are perfect for a day of sun and relaxation. Visitors can also take a stroll through the city's historic streets, or explore the local markets and shops.

Overall, Heraklion is a city that is full of surprises and delights. Its unique blend of history, culture, and cuisine make it a must-visit destination for any budget traveller looking for an unforgettable experience.
