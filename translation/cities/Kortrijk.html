Kortrijk

Kortrijk, a charming city in the Flemish region of Belgium, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. With its rich history and stunning architecture, Kortrijk is a city that will leave you in awe. The city is known for its beautiful medieval buildings, such as the Broel Towers and the Gothic-style St. Martin's Church, which are a testament to its rich cultural heritage.

Kortrijk is also famous for its vibrant festivals, including the annual Kortrijk Congé, which celebrates the city's cultural diversity with music, dance, and food. The city is also home to a thriving art scene, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the works of local artists.

One of the most surprising facts about Kortrijk is that it was once a major center of the textile industry, and you can still see the remnants of this history in the city's many textile museums and factories. Another interesting fact is that Kortrijk is home to the famous Belgian dish, stoofvlees, a hearty beef stew that is a must-try for any foodie.

For budget travellers, Kortrijk offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions, such as the beautiful Begijnhof, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the stunning Belfry, which offers breathtaking views of the city. The city's many parks and gardens are also perfect for a relaxing afternoon stroll.

In contrast to the hustle and bustle of larger cities, Kortrijk offers a peaceful and laid-back atmosphere that is perfect for those seeking a slower pace of life. With its unique blend of history, culture, and natural beauty, Kortrijk is a city that will capture your heart and leave you wanting more.
