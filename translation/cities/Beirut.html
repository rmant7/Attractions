Beirut

Beirut, the capital city of Lebanon, is a vibrant and bustling destination that offers a unique blend of history, culture, and modernity. This city is a perfect destination for budget travellers who want to experience the Middle Eastern charm without breaking the bank. Beirut is a city that is rich in culture and traditions, and it is evident in its architecture, festivals, and art.

The city is a melting pot of different cultures, and this is reflected in its architecture. The city has a mix of modern and traditional buildings, and the contrast is striking. The city is also home to several festivals throughout the year, including the Beirut International Film Festival and the Beirut Art Fair.

Lebanese cuisine is famous all over the world, and Beirut is the perfect place to indulge in some of the best national foods. From the famous shawarma to the delicious hummus, the city has something to offer for every food lover. The city is also home to several street food vendors, where you can try some of the local delicacies at a low cost.

Beirut is a city that is full of surprises. Did you know that the city has the oldest continuously inhabited city in the world? The city is also home to several museums, including the National Museum of Beirut, which houses some of the most significant archaeological finds in the country.

For budget travellers, there are several free or low-cost attractions in the city. The Corniche is a beautiful promenade that offers stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea. The city is also home to several parks, including the Horsh Beirut, which is the largest park in the city.

In conclusion, Beirut is a city that is full of life and energy. It is a perfect destination for budget travellers who want to experience the Middle Eastern charm without breaking the bank. The city's culture, traditions, architecture, festivals, art, and national food are sure to leave a lasting impression on any visitor.
