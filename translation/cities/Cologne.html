Cologne

Cologne, the fourth-largest city in Germany, is a vibrant and exciting destination for budget travellers. This city is steeped in history and culture, with a rich architectural heritage that spans centuries. From the towering spires of the iconic Cologne Cathedral to the charming cobblestone streets of the Old Town, there is no shortage of stunning sights to explore.

Cologne is also known for its lively festivals and events, including the world-famous Carnival celebrations that take place every February. This colourful and exuberant festival is a must-see for anyone visiting the city, with parades, music, and dancing filling the streets for days on end.

Art lovers will also find plenty to enjoy in Cologne, with a range of museums and galleries showcasing everything from contemporary art to ancient artefacts. The Museum Ludwig is a particular highlight, with an impressive collection of modern and contemporary art that includes works by Picasso, Warhol, and Lichtenstein.

Of course, no visit to Cologne would be complete without sampling some of the city's delicious national food. From hearty sausages and sauerkraut to sweet and sticky pastries, there is something to suit every taste and budget.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Cologne. The city's many parks and gardens are perfect for a leisurely stroll, while the Rhine River offers stunning views and a chance to relax by the water.

One of the most surprising facts about Cologne is that it is home to the largest number of breweries in Germany, with over 20 different breweries producing a range of delicious beers. Visitors can take a tour of one of these breweries to learn more about the brewing process and sample some of the local brews.

Overall, Cologne is a city that offers something for everyone, with a unique blend of history, culture, and modernity that is sure to captivate and inspire.
