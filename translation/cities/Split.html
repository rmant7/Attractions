Split

Split, the second-largest city in Croatia, is a budget traveller's paradise. This vibrant city is a perfect blend of ancient history and modern culture, making it an ideal destination for those who want to experience the best of both worlds. The city is known for its stunning architecture, including the iconic Diocletian's Palace, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The palace is a perfect example of Roman architecture and is a must-visit for anyone interested in history.

Split is also famous for its rich culture and traditions. The city has a vibrant arts scene, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the works of local artists. The city is also home to several festivals throughout the year, including the Split Summer Festival, which features music, theatre, and dance performances.

One of the most exciting things about Split is its delicious national food. The city is known for its fresh seafood, grilled meats, and traditional Dalmatian dishes. Visitors can enjoy local delicacies at affordable prices in the city's many restaurants and cafes.

Split is also full of surprises. Did you know that the city has a beach right in the city center? Bacvice Beach is a popular spot for locals and tourists alike, and it's the perfect place to relax and soak up the sun. Another surprising fact is that Split has a thriving street art scene, with murals and graffiti adorning the city's walls.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions in Split. Visitors can explore the city's historic old town, take a stroll along the Riva promenade, or hike up Marjan Hill for stunning views of the city and the Adriatic Sea.

In conclusion, Split is a city that has something for everyone. Its rich history, vibrant culture, delicious food, and stunning architecture make it a must-visit destination for budget travellers. So pack your bags and get ready to experience the best of Croatia in this beautiful city.
