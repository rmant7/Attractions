Baghdad

Baghdad, the capital city of Iraq, is a fascinating destination for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. This ancient city is steeped in history, with a rich tapestry of traditions, architecture, and art that will leave you in awe. The city's vibrant culture is reflected in its festivals, which are celebrated with great enthusiasm and fervour. The most famous of these is the Baghdad International Festival, which showcases the best of Iraqi music, dance, and theatre.

The city's architecture is a blend of ancient and modern styles, with towering skyscrapers and ancient mosques coexisting side by side. The most famous of these is the Al-Mustansiriya School, a stunning example of Islamic architecture that dates back to the 13th century. The city's art scene is equally impressive, with a thriving community of artists and galleries showcasing everything from traditional calligraphy to contemporary installations.

No visit to Baghdad is complete without sampling the city's national food, which is a delicious blend of Middle Eastern and Mediterranean flavours. From succulent kebabs to fragrant rice dishes, the food here is sure to tantalize your taste buds. And if you're looking for free or low-cost attractions, there are plenty of options to choose from. The Baghdad Zoo, for example, is a great place to spend an afternoon, while the Al-Shaheed Monument offers stunning views of the city.

Despite its troubled past, Baghdad is a city that is full of surprises and intrigue. From its bustling markets to its ancient ruins, there is always something new to discover here. So why not pack your bags and experience the magic of this incredible city for yourself?
