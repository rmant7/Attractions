Kuwait City

Kuwait City is a vibrant and bustling metropolis that offers budget travellers a unique blend of modernity and tradition. The city is steeped in rich culture and traditions that are reflected in its stunning architecture, festivals, art, and national food. The city's skyline is dominated by towering skyscrapers that are a testament to its rapid development, but it also boasts a wealth of historical landmarks that showcase its rich heritage.

One of the most striking features of Kuwait City is its stunning Islamic architecture, which is a blend of traditional and modern styles. The Grand Mosque is a must-visit attraction for anyone interested in Islamic art and architecture. The city is also home to several museums and art galleries that showcase the country's rich cultural heritage.

Kuwait City is known for its vibrant festivals and celebrations, which are a reflection of its diverse cultural heritage. The Kuwaiti National Day is a particularly exciting event that celebrates the country's independence and is marked by parades, fireworks, and cultural performances.

The city's national food is a delicious blend of Arabic, Indian, and Persian cuisine, with dishes like machboos, biryani, and kebabs being popular among locals and tourists alike. The city also has a thriving street food scene, with vendors selling everything from shawarma to falafel.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Kuwait City. The Kuwait Towers offer stunning views of the city and are a popular spot for picnics and photo ops. The Souq Al-Mubarakiya is a bustling market that offers a glimpse into the city's traditional way of life, with vendors selling everything from spices to textiles.

Overall, Kuwait City is a fascinating destination that offers budget travellers a unique blend of modernity and tradition. Its rich culture, stunning architecture, vibrant festivals, and delicious food make it a must-visit destination for anyone looking to explore the Middle East.
