Palma de Mallorca

Palma de Mallorca is a vibrant city that offers budget travellers a unique blend of culture, history, and natural beauty. The city is known for its stunning architecture, which ranges from Gothic to modernist styles. The city's rich history is evident in its many museums, galleries, and historic landmarks, such as the stunning Palma Cathedral.

Palma de Mallorca is also famous for its festivals, which are celebrated throughout the year. The city's most famous festival is the Festa de Sant Joan, which takes place in June and features fireworks, bonfires, and traditional dancing. The city is also home to a thriving arts scene, with many galleries and exhibitions showcasing the work of local artists.

One of the most surprising facts about Palma de Mallorca is that it is home to some of the best beaches in Europe. The city's beaches are known for their crystal-clear waters and soft white sand, making them the perfect place to relax and soak up the sun.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Palma de Mallorca. The city's historic old town is a must-see, with its narrow streets, charming squares, and beautiful architecture. The city's many parks and gardens are also a great place to relax and enjoy the natural beauty of the island.

When it comes to food, Palma de Mallorca is famous for its delicious seafood dishes, such as paella and grilled octopus. The city also has a thriving street food scene, with many vendors selling traditional snacks and treats.

Overall, Palma de Mallorca is a city that offers something for everyone. Whether you're interested in history, culture, or simply relaxing on the beach, this beautiful city is the perfect destination for budget travellers looking for an unforgettable experience.
