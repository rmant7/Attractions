Ashgabat

Ashgabat, the capital city of Turkmenistan, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. The city is a blend of modernity and tradition, with its stunning architecture and rich history. The city's unique white marble buildings and monuments are a sight to behold, and the city's layout is designed to showcase its grandeur.

Ashgabat is a city that is steeped in tradition, and its people are proud of their heritage. The city's festivals and celebrations are a testament to this, with events such as the Turkmenistan Independence Day and the Nowruz Festival being celebrated with great enthusiasm. The city's art scene is also thriving, with many galleries showcasing the works of local artists.

The national food of Ashgabat is a delicious blend of Middle Eastern and Central Asian cuisine. The city's markets are a great place to sample the local delicacies, such as plov, a rice dish with meat and vegetables, and shashlik, grilled meat skewers. The city's tea culture is also worth experiencing, with many tea houses serving traditional Turkmen tea.

One of the most surprising facts about Ashgabat is that it holds the Guinness World Record for having the most white marble buildings in the world. The city is also home to the world's largest indoor Ferris wheel, which offers stunning views of the city.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Ashgabat. The city's parks and gardens are a great place to relax and take in the city's beauty, and the Ashgabat National Museum of History and Ethnography is a must-visit for those interested in the city's rich history.

In conclusion, Ashgabat is a city that offers a unique blend of culture, tradition, and modernity. Its stunning architecture, delicious food, and vibrant festivals make it a must-visit destination for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience.
