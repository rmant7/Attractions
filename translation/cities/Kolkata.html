Kolkata

Kolkata, the cultural capital of India, is a city that never fails to mesmerize its visitors. With its rich history, vibrant culture, and stunning architecture, Kolkata is a must-visit destination for budget travellers. The city is known for its unique blend of colonial and Indian architecture, which can be seen in its iconic landmarks such as the Victoria Memorial, St. Paul's Cathedral, and the Howrah Bridge.

Kolkata is also famous for its festivals, which are celebrated with great enthusiasm and fervour. The Durga Puja festival, which takes place in October, is one of the biggest and most colourful festivals in India. During this time, the city comes alive with colourful lights, music, and dance performances.

The city is also home to some of the finest art galleries and museums in the country. The Indian Museum, the Academy of Fine Arts, and the Victoria Memorial Hall are just a few of the many places where visitors can explore the rich cultural heritage of Kolkata.

Kolkata is also known for its delicious cuisine, which is a blend of Indian, Chinese, and European influences. Some of the must-try dishes include the famous Kolkata biryani, fish curry, and rasgulla.

One of the most surprising facts about Kolkata is that it was once the capital of British India. The city has a rich history, which can be seen in its many heritage buildings and monuments.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Kolkata. The Maidan, a large urban park, is a great place to relax and enjoy the city's greenery. The Dakshineswar Kali Temple, the Kalighat Temple, and the Belur Math are some of the many religious sites that can be visited for free.

In conclusion, Kolkata is a city that offers a unique blend of culture, history, and architecture. With its vibrant festivals, delicious cuisine, and stunning landmarks, it is a must-visit destination for budget travellers. So, pack your bags and get ready to explore the city's many wonders!
