Burgas

Burgas, a hidden gem on the Bulgarian Black Sea coast, is a budget traveller's paradise. This vibrant city boasts a rich cultural heritage, with a blend of ancient and modern architecture that will leave you in awe. The city's unique blend of cultures is reflected in its festivals, art, and national food, which are sure to tantalize your taste buds.

Burgas is a city that surprises and intrigues visitors with its many hidden gems. Did you know that the city is home to the largest salt pans in Bulgaria? These salt pans have been in operation for over a century and are a must-visit for anyone interested in the city's history. Another hidden gem is the Burgas Regional Historical Museum, which houses a vast collection of artefacts from the city's past.

For budget travellers, Burgas offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions. Take a stroll along the beautiful Sea Garden, a park that stretches along the coast and offers stunning views of the sea. Visit the Ethnographic Museum, which showcases the city's traditional way of life. Or, head to the Burgas Art Gallery, which features works by local artists.

Burgas is also known for its delicious national food, which is a blend of Bulgarian and Mediterranean cuisine. Try the traditional Shopska salad, made with tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers, and feta cheese. Or, sample the local fish dishes, which are caught fresh from the Black Sea.

One of the city's most popular festivals is the Sand Fest, which takes place every summer. This festival sees artists from around the world come together to create stunning sand sculptures on the beach. Another festival not to be missed is the Burgas International Film Festival, which showcases the best of Bulgarian and international cinema.

In conclusion, Burgas is a city that offers something for everyone. With its rich cultural heritage, stunning architecture, delicious food, and exciting festivals, it is a must-visit destination for budget travellers. So, pack your bags and head to Burgas for an unforgettable experience!
