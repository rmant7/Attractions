Brussels

Brussels, the capital city of Belgium, is a vibrant and exciting destination for budget travellers. This city is a melting pot of cultures, traditions, and architecture, making it a unique and fascinating place to explore. The city is known for its stunning Gothic architecture, which can be seen in the Grand Place, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The city is also home to many museums and galleries, showcasing the best of Belgian art and culture.

One of the most exciting things about Brussels is its festivals. The city is famous for its beer festivals, including the Brussels Beer Festival, which attracts thousands of visitors every year. The city is also home to the famous Flower Carpet, a biennial event where the Grand Place is covered in a stunning carpet of flowers.

Belgium is known for its delicious food, and Brussels is no exception. The city is famous for its waffles, chocolate, and fries, which can be found at street vendors and cafes throughout the city. For those looking for a more traditional meal, Brussels is also home to many restaurants serving classic Belgian dishes like moules-frites (mussels and fries) and carbonnade flamande (beef stew).

Brussels is full of surprises, including the famous Manneken Pis statue, a small bronze statue of a boy peeing, which has become a symbol of the city. The city is also home to the Atomium, a unique building shaped like an atom, which offers stunning views of the city.

For budget travellers, there are many free or low-cost attractions in Brussels. The city is home to many beautiful parks, including the Parc du Cinquantenaire, which offers stunning views of the city. The city also has many free museums, including the Museum of the City of Brussels and the Royal Museums of Fine Arts of Belgium.

In conclusion, Brussels is a city full of culture, traditions, and surprises, making it an exciting destination for budget travellers. From its stunning architecture to its delicious food and festivals, there is something for everyone in this vibrant city. So why not pack your bags and explore all that Brussels has to offer?
