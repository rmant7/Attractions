Tallinn

Tallinn, the capital city of Estonia, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic European experience. This charming city boasts a rich history and culture, with a unique blend of Scandinavian and Russian influences. The Old Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is a must-see for its stunning medieval architecture and cobblestone streets. Visitors can explore the city's fascinating past at the Estonian History Museum or admire the contemporary art at the Kumu Art Museum.

Tallinn is also known for its vibrant festivals, including the Medieval Days and Christmas Market, where visitors can indulge in traditional Estonian food and drink. Speaking of food, Tallinn's cuisine is a delicious fusion of Baltic, Russian, and Scandinavian flavors. Be sure to try the national dish, black bread, and smoked fish.

For those on a budget, Tallinn offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions, such as the Kadriorg Park and Palace, the Tallinn Botanic Garden, and the stunning views from Toompea Hill. Visitors can also take a stroll along the picturesque coastline or explore the city's many parks and gardens.

What sets Tallinn apart from other European cities is its unique blend of old and new. The city's modern skyline contrasts with its historic architecture, creating a truly unique atmosphere. And did you know that Tallinn was the first city in the world to offer free public Wi-Fi? It's no wonder that Tallinn has been named one of the most digitally advanced cities in the world.

In summary, Tallinn is a budget-friendly destination that offers a rich cultural experience, delicious cuisine, and stunning architecture. With its unique blend of old and new, this city is sure to captivate visitors and leave them with unforgettable memories.
