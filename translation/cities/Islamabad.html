Islamabad

Islamabad, the capital city of Pakistan, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. The city boasts a rich history and a unique blend of modern and traditional architecture. The stunning Faisal Mosque, with its iconic white marble structure, is a must-visit for anyone interested in Islamic art and architecture.

Islamabad is a melting pot of cultures and traditions, with a vibrant arts scene and a plethora of festivals throughout the year. The annual Lok Mela festival showcases the best of Pakistani folk art, music, and dance, while the Islamabad Literature Festival attracts renowned writers and intellectuals from around the world.

Foodies will be delighted by the city's diverse culinary scene, with mouth-watering street food and traditional dishes like biryani and kebabs. For a truly authentic experience, head to the bustling food markets in the old city and sample the local delicacies.

Despite being a modern city, Islamabad is surrounded by breathtaking natural beauty. The Margalla Hills National Park offers stunning hiking trails and panoramic views of the city, while the nearby Rawal Lake is a popular spot for picnics and boating.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore. The Pakistan Monument and Museum showcases the country's rich history and culture, while the Shakarparian Hills offer stunning views of the city and the surrounding mountains.

In contrast to the hustle and bustle of other South Asian cities, Islamabad is a peaceful and serene oasis. The city's wide boulevards, lush green parks, and clean streets make it a refreshing change from the chaos of other urban centres.

Overall, Islamabad is a city that surprises and intrigues visitors with its unique blend of culture, history, and natural beauty. Whether you're a budget traveller or a luxury seeker, this city has something to offer everyone.
