Krakow

Krakow, the cultural capital of Poland, is a must-visit destination for budget travellers. This charming city is steeped in history and boasts a rich cultural heritage that is sure to captivate visitors. From its stunning architecture to its vibrant festivals, Krakow has something for everyone.

The city's Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and is home to some of the most beautiful buildings in Europe. The Gothic St. Mary's Basilica and the Renaissance Cloth Hall are just two of the many architectural wonders that can be found here. The city's Jewish Quarter, Kazimierz, is also a must-visit, with its cobbled streets and historic synagogues.

Krakow is a city that loves to celebrate, and there are plenty of festivals and events throughout the year. The annual Krakow Film Festival and the Jewish Culture Festival are just two of the many events that take place here. The city is also home to a thriving arts scene, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the work of local artists.

No visit to Krakow would be complete without sampling some of the city's delicious national food. Pierogi, a type of dumpling, is a local favourite, as is kielbasa, a type of sausage. For those with a sweet tooth, the city's famous obwarzanek, a type of pretzel, is a must-try.

Krakow is also home to some surprising facts. Did you know that the city's Wawel Castle was once the residence of Polish kings? Or that the city's Main Market Square is one of the largest medieval squares in Europe?

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Krakow. The city's many parks and gardens are perfect for a leisurely stroll, while the Schindler Factory Museum offers a fascinating insight into the city's history.

In conclusion, Krakow is a city that is sure to enchant and delight budget travellers. With its rich cultural heritage, stunning architecture, and vibrant festivals, there is something for everyone here. So why not book your trip today and discover the magic of Krakow for yourself?
