Eindhoven

Eindhoven, a vibrant city in the southern Netherlands, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic Dutch experience. Known as the "City of Light," Eindhoven boasts a rich history of innovation and design, with a thriving tech industry and a bustling creative scene. The city's modern architecture, including the iconic Evoluon building, contrasts beautifully with its historic landmarks, such as the 13th-century St. Catherine's Church.

Eindhoven is also home to a diverse range of cultural events and festivals throughout the year, including the annual Dutch Design Week and the GLOW light festival. The city's art scene is equally impressive, with world-class museums such as the Van Abbemuseum showcasing contemporary and modern art.

For foodies, Eindhoven offers a taste of traditional Dutch cuisine, with hearty dishes such as stamppot and bitterballen. But the city also boasts a multicultural food scene, with a range of international restaurants and street food vendors.

Surprising facts about Eindhoven include its status as the birthplace of Philips electronics and the location of the world's first light bulb factory. Visitors can explore this history at the Philips Museum, which offers free admission on the last Sunday of every month.

Other free or low-cost attractions in Eindhoven include the beautiful Genneper Parken, a sprawling nature reserve with hiking trails and a petting zoo, and the Strijp-S district, a former industrial site turned creative hub with street art and trendy cafes.

Overall, Eindhoven offers budget travellers a unique blend of history, culture, and innovation, with plenty of affordable attractions to explore.
