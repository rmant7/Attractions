Skopje

Skopje, the capital city of North Macedonia, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. This vibrant city is a melting pot of cultures, traditions, and architecture, with a rich history dating back to the Roman Empire. Skopje is a city of contrasts, where ancient ruins and modern buildings coexist in harmony.

The city is known for its lively festivals, such as the Skopje Jazz Festival and the Skopje Summer Festival, which showcase the best of Macedonian music, art, and culture. The city's art scene is also thriving, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing contemporary and traditional Macedonian art.

Skopje's architecture is a blend of Ottoman, Byzantine, and modern styles, with the iconic Stone Bridge and the Kale Fortress being must-see attractions. The city's national food is a delicious blend of Mediterranean and Balkan cuisine, with dishes such as ajvar, burek, and tavche gravche being popular among locals and tourists alike.

One of the most surprising facts about Skopje is that it was almost completely destroyed by an earthquake in 1963, and has since been rebuilt with a modern twist. The city is also home to the largest statue of a warrior on a horse in the world, the Alexander the Great statue.

For budget travellers, Skopje offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions, such as the Old Bazaar, the City Park, and the Museum of the Macedonian Struggle for Independence. The city's vibrant nightlife scene is also affordable, with numerous bars and clubs offering cheap drinks and live music.

In Skopje, visitors can immerse themselves in a unique cultural experience, where ancient history meets modernity, and traditional values meet contemporary art. This city is a must-visit destination for budget travellers seeking an authentic and unforgettable adventure.
