Funchal

Funchal, the capital city of Madeira, is a budget traveller's paradise. This charming city is a perfect blend of old-world charm and modern amenities. The city is known for its vibrant culture, rich traditions, and stunning architecture. Funchal is a city that is steeped in history, and it is evident in the city's architecture, which is a mix of Gothic, Baroque, and Renaissance styles.

The city is also famous for its festivals, which are a celebration of its rich culture and traditions. The most popular festival is the Funchal Carnival, which is a colourful and lively event that takes place in February. The city is also home to several museums and art galleries, which showcase the city's rich history and culture.

One of the most surprising facts about Funchal is that it is home to the world's largest fireworks display, which takes place every New Year's Eve. The city is also known for its delicious national food, which includes fresh seafood, grilled meats, and traditional Madeiran dishes.

For budget travellers, Funchal offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions, such as the beautiful Jardim Botânico, which is a stunning botanical garden that offers breathtaking views of the city. The city also has several beautiful parks and beaches, which are perfect for a relaxing day out.

In conclusion, Funchal is a city that is full of surprises and delights. Its rich culture, stunning architecture, and delicious food make it a must-visit destination for budget travellers. Whether you're looking for a relaxing day out or an exciting adventure, Funchal has something for everyone.
