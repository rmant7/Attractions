Olbia

Olbia, a charming city located in the heart of Sardinia, is a perfect destination for budget travellers seeking an authentic Italian experience. This city is rich in culture, traditions, and architecture that will leave you in awe. The city's ancient history is reflected in its stunning architecture, including the Romanesque Church of San Simplicio and the medieval Castle of Pedres. Olbia is also famous for its vibrant festivals, such as the Feast of San Simplicio, which takes place in May and features traditional Sardinian music, dance, and food.

The city's art scene is also worth exploring, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing contemporary and traditional Sardinian art. The National Archaeological Museum of Olbia is a must-visit for history buffs, featuring a vast collection of ancient artifacts and exhibits. The city's national food is a treat for the taste buds, with dishes like culurgiones, a type of ravioli filled with potatoes, cheese, and mint, and seadas, a dessert made with cheese and honey.

Olbia is also home to some surprising natural wonders, such as the stunning beaches of Costa Smeralda and the nearby Maddalena Archipelago. These attractions are free to visit and offer breathtaking views of the crystal-clear waters and rugged coastline. For those seeking a more active adventure, Olbia offers hiking trails, cycling routes, and water sports activities.

In contrast to the bustling cities of Italy, Olbia offers a laid-back and relaxed atmosphere, making it an ideal destination for budget travellers seeking a peaceful getaway. The city's unique blend of ancient history, vibrant culture, and stunning natural beauty makes it a must-visit destination for anyone travelling to Sardinia.
