Palanga

Palanga is a charming coastal city located in the western part of Lithuania, known for its stunning beaches, rich cultural heritage, and vibrant nightlife. This city is a perfect destination for budget travellers who want to experience the best of Lithuania without breaking the bank.

Palanga is a city that is steeped in history and culture, with a unique blend of Lithuanian and German influences. The city's architecture is a testament to its rich history, with beautiful buildings and monuments that date back to the 19th century.

One of the most exciting things about Palanga is its festivals and events. The city hosts a variety of cultural events throughout the year, including the Palanga Summer Festival, which features music, dance, and theatre performances.

Palanga is also known for its delicious national food, which includes traditional Lithuanian dishes such as cepelinai (potato dumplings), kugelis (potato pudding), and šaltibarščiai (cold beet soup).

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Palanga. The city's beautiful beaches are a must-visit, and there are also several parks and gardens to explore. The Palanga Amber Museum is another popular attraction, which showcases the city's rich history and culture.

One of the most surprising things about Palanga is its nightlife. Despite being a small city, Palanga has a vibrant and lively nightlife scene, with plenty of bars, clubs, and restaurants to choose from.

In conclusion, Palanga is a city that offers something for everyone, from its rich cultural heritage to its stunning beaches and vibrant nightlife. Whether you're a budget traveller or not, Palanga is a destination that should be on your travel bucket list.
