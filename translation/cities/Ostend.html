Ostend

Ostend, a charming coastal city in Belgium, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking a unique cultural experience. With its rich history and stunning architecture, Ostend is a city that will leave you in awe. The city is known for its vibrant festivals, including the Ostend at Anchor festival, which celebrates the city's maritime heritage. The city's art scene is also thriving, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the works of local artists.

Ostend's cuisine is a must-try for foodies on a budget. The city is famous for its seafood, with fresh catches of shrimp, mussels, and oysters available at affordable prices. The national dish of Belgium, moules-frites (mussels and fries), is a must-try when visiting Ostend.

One of the most surprising facts about Ostend is that it was once a popular destination for royalty, including Queen Victoria and King Leopold II. The city's grand architecture, including the Royal Galleries, is a testament to its royal past.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Ostend. The beach is a popular spot for sunbathing and swimming, while the city's parks and gardens offer a peaceful escape from the hustle and bustle of the city. The Mu.ZEE museum is also free to visit on the first Sunday of every month.

Ostend's unique features are best highlighted by comparing and contrasting it with other coastal cities. Unlike other popular beach destinations, Ostend has managed to maintain its authentic charm and character, making it a refreshing change from the typical tourist traps.

In conclusion, Ostend is a city that offers budget travellers a unique cultural experience. From its stunning architecture to its vibrant festivals and delicious cuisine, there is something for everyone in this charming coastal city. So pack your bags and head to Ostend for an unforgettable adventure.
