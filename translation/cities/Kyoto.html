Kyoto

Kyoto, the cultural capital of Japan, is a must-visit destination for budget travellers. This city is a perfect blend of ancient traditions and modernity, making it a unique and exciting place to explore. Kyoto is home to over 2,000 temples and shrines, each with its own story and significance. The city's architecture is a mix of traditional Japanese and modern styles, creating a beautiful and diverse landscape.

One of the most exciting things about Kyoto is its festivals. The city hosts several festivals throughout the year, including the famous Gion Matsuri, which takes place in July. During this festival, the streets are filled with colourful floats, traditional music, and delicious food.

Kyoto is also known for its art and culture. The city is home to several museums and galleries, showcasing traditional Japanese art and contemporary works. The National Museum of Modern Art is a must-visit for art lovers.

When it comes to food, Kyoto has a lot to offer. The city is famous for its Kaiseki cuisine, a traditional multi-course meal that is both delicious and visually stunning. Other must-try dishes include ramen, sushi, and tempura.

One surprising fact about Kyoto is that it was once the capital of Japan. The city has a rich history, and visitors can explore the ancient imperial palace and other historical sites.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions in Kyoto. The Fushimi Inari Shrine, with its thousands of torii gates, is a popular destination that is free to visit. The Arashiyama Bamboo Grove is another must-see attraction that is free to explore.

In conclusion, Kyoto is a city that offers something for everyone. Its rich culture, traditions, architecture, festivals, art, and food make it a unique and exciting destination. Whether you're a budget traveller or not, Kyoto is a city that should be on your travel bucket list.
