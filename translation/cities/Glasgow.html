Glasgow

Glasgow, the largest city in Scotland, is a vibrant and exciting destination for budget travellers. This city is known for its rich culture, traditions, and stunning architecture that will leave you in awe. Glasgow is a city that is full of life, with a bustling atmosphere that is sure to captivate your senses.

The city is home to a variety of festivals and events throughout the year, including the Glasgow International Comedy Festival, the Glasgow Film Festival, and the Celtic Connections music festival. These events showcase the city's love for the arts and its vibrant cultural scene.

Glasgow is also known for its delicious national food, including haggis, neeps, and tatties. This hearty dish is a must-try for any visitor to the city. The city's food scene is diverse, with plenty of options for budget travellers to enjoy.

One of the most surprising facts about Glasgow is that it has more green space per capita than any other city in Europe. This means that there are plenty of free and low-cost attractions for visitors to enjoy, including the Kelvingrove Art Gallery and Museum, the Glasgow Botanic Gardens, and the Riverside Museum.

The city's architecture is a unique blend of old and new, with stunning Victorian buildings standing alongside modern structures. The contrast between the two is striking and adds to the city's charm.

Glasgow is a city that is full of surprises and is sure to leave a lasting impression on any visitor. With its rich culture, stunning architecture, and vibrant atmosphere, it is a must-visit destination for budget travellers looking for an unforgettable experience.
