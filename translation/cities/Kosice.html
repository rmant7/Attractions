Kosice

Kosice, the second-largest city in Slovakia, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic European experience. This vibrant city boasts a rich history and culture, with stunning architecture that dates back to the medieval times. The city's Gothic St. Elisabeth Cathedral is a must-see attraction, with its towering spires and intricate carvings.

Kosice is also known for its lively festivals, including the famous Kosice Peace Marathon and the International Festival of Eastern Slovak Folklore. These events showcase the city's unique traditions and customs, which are deeply rooted in its history and heritage.

The city's art scene is also thriving, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the works of local and international artists. The East Slovak Gallery is a particular highlight, with its impressive collection of contemporary art.

Foodies will love Kosice's national cuisine, which is a delicious blend of Hungarian, Slovak, and Ukrainian influences. Traditional dishes include bryndzové halušky (potato dumplings with sheep cheese) and goulash (a hearty stew made with beef and vegetables).

Surprising facts about Kosice include its status as the European Capital of Culture in 2013, and its designation as the first Slovakian city to have a public transport system powered entirely by electricity.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Kosice. The city's beautiful parks and gardens are perfect for a leisurely stroll, while the historic Old Town is a treasure trove of architectural wonders.

In contrast to other European cities, Kosice offers a unique blend of old-world charm and modern amenities, making it an ideal destination for travellers seeking an authentic and affordable European experience.
