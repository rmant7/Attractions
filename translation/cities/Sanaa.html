Sanaa

Sanaa, the capital city of Yemen, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. The city is known for its stunning architecture, with towering mud-brick buildings that have stood for centuries. The Old City of Sanaa is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and wandering through its narrow streets is like stepping back in time. The city's rich history and traditions are evident in its vibrant festivals, such as the annual Eid al-Fitr celebration, where locals gather to feast and dance.

The art scene in Sanaa is also thriving, with many local artists showcasing their work in galleries and markets throughout the city. The national food of Yemen, such as the popular dish of fahsa, is a must-try for any foodie. The dish consists of slow-cooked lamb or beef in a spicy tomato sauce, served with bread and vegetables.

One of the most surprising facts about Sanaa is that it sits at an altitude of over 7,000 feet, making it one of the highest capital cities in the world. This altitude also means that the city enjoys a cooler climate than many other parts of Yemen, making it a pleasant escape from the heat.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Sanaa. The National Museum of Yemen is a great place to learn about the country's history and culture, while the Al Saleh Mosque is a stunning example of modern Islamic architecture. The Bab al-Yemen gate is also a must-see, with its intricate carvings and imposing presence.

In contrast to the bustling streets of the Old City, the modern part of Sanaa offers a different experience, with shopping malls, cinemas, and restaurants. However, the charm of the Old City is hard to resist, with its unique architecture and bustling markets.

Overall, Sanaa is a city that appeals to the senses and emotions, with its rich culture, stunning architecture, and delicious food. For budget travellers seeking an authentic experience, Sanaa is a destination not to be missed.
