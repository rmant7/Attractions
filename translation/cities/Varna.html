Varna

Varna, the third-largest city in Bulgaria, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. This coastal city boasts a rich history and a vibrant atmosphere that will leave you enchanted. The city is known for its stunning architecture, with a mix of ancient ruins, Ottoman-era buildings, and modern structures. The Roman Baths, the Cathedral of the Assumption of the Virgin, and the Sea Garden are just a few of the must-see attractions.

Varna is also a hub for art and culture, with numerous museums and galleries showcasing local and international artists. The Varna International Ballet Competition, held every two years, is a world-renowned event that attracts dancers from all over the globe. The city is also famous for its delicious cuisine, with traditional Bulgarian dishes like banitsa, kebapche, and shopska salad that will tantalize your taste buds.

One of the most surprising facts about Varna is that it is home to the oldest gold treasure in the world, dating back to 4,600 BC. The treasure is on display at the Varna Archaeological Museum, which is a must-visit for history buffs. Another unique feature of Varna is its proximity to the Black Sea, with miles of sandy beaches and crystal-clear waters that are perfect for swimming, sunbathing, and water sports.

For budget travellers, Varna offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions, such as the Sea Garden, the Ethnographic Museum, and the Naval Museum. The city also has a vibrant nightlife scene, with numerous bars and clubs that cater to all tastes and budgets. Whether you're looking for a relaxing beach holiday or an exciting cultural adventure, Varna has something for everyone. So pack your bags and get ready to discover this hidden gem on the Black Sea coast.
