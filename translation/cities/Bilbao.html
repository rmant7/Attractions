Bilbao

Bilbao, the largest city in the Basque Country, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. This vibrant city is known for its unique blend of modern and traditional architecture, with the iconic Guggenheim Museum Bilbao being a must-see attraction. But Bilbao is more than just a museum city, it is a city of festivals, art, and delicious food.

The Basque culture is deeply rooted in Bilbao, and visitors can immerse themselves in the local traditions by attending one of the many festivals held throughout the year. The Semana Grande, or Big Week, is a week-long celebration of Basque culture, with music, dance, and fireworks lighting up the city. The Basque Museum and Cultural Center is also a great place to learn about the history and traditions of the Basque people.

Bilbao's architecture is a mix of old and new, with the Casco Viejo, or Old Town, being a prime example of the city's historic charm. The narrow streets and colorful buildings are a stark contrast to the modern architecture of the Guggenheim Museum. The Zubizuri Bridge, designed by Santiago Calatrava, is another example of Bilbao's modern architecture and is a popular spot for photos.

Art lovers will be in heaven in Bilbao, with the Guggenheim Museum being just the tip of the iceberg. The Bilbao Fine Arts Museum houses an impressive collection of Spanish and Basque art, while the Azkuna Zentroa is a cultural center that hosts exhibitions, concerts, and other events.

No visit to Bilbao is complete without trying the local cuisine. Basque cuisine is known for its fresh seafood, pintxos (small snacks), and hearty stews. The Mercado de la Ribera is a great place to sample the local fare, with vendors selling everything from fresh produce to pintxos.

Surprising facts about Bilbao include the fact that it was once a major industrial city, but has since transformed into a cultural hub. The city is also home to the world's first commercial electric tramway, which began operating in 1896.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions in Bilbao. The Casco Viejo is a great place to wander and explore, while the Artxanda Funicular offers stunning views of the city for a small fee. The Basque Museum and Cultural Center is also free to enter.

In conclusion, Bilbao is a city that offers something for everyone, from its unique architecture to its rich culture and delicious food. It is a city that surprises and intrigues, and is a must-visit destination for budget travellers looking for an authentic cultural experience.
