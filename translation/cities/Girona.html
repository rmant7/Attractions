Girona

Girona, a charming city in Catalonia, Spain, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic Spanish experience. The city is steeped in history and culture, with its medieval architecture and winding streets transporting visitors back in time. The city's rich history is evident in its ancient walls, Gothic cathedral, and Jewish quarter, which is one of the best-preserved in Europe.

Girona is also known for its vibrant festivals, including the Temps de Flors, a flower festival that transforms the city into a colourful wonderland. The city's art scene is also thriving, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing local and international talent.

Foodies will be delighted by Girona's culinary offerings, which include traditional Catalan dishes such as paella, tapas, and seafood. The city is also home to several Michelin-starred restaurants, making it a food lover's paradise.

One of the most surprising facts about Girona is that it was used as a filming location for the hit TV series Game of Thrones. Fans of the show can visit the city's medieval walls, which were used as the backdrop for several scenes.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Girona. The city's beautiful parks and gardens are perfect for a picnic or a leisurely stroll, while the River Onyar offers stunning views of the city's colourful houses.

In contrast to the bustling streets of Barcelona, Girona offers a more relaxed and authentic Spanish experience. With its rich history, vibrant culture, and delicious food, Girona is a must-visit destination for budget travellers looking to explore the best of Spain.
