Kathmandu

Kathmandu, the capital city of Nepal, is a budget traveller's paradise. This vibrant city is a melting pot of cultures, traditions, and architecture that will leave you in awe. The city is home to ancient temples, palaces, and stupas that are a testament to its rich history and heritage. The city's unique blend of Hindu and Buddhist cultures is evident in its festivals, art, and national food.

The city's most famous landmark is the Swayambhunath Stupa, also known as the Monkey Temple, which offers stunning views of the city. Another must-visit attraction is the Pashupatinath Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the most sacred Hindu temples in the world. The temple is located on the banks of the Bagmati River and is a hub of religious activity.

Kathmandu is also known for its vibrant festivals, including the colorful Holi festival and the mesmerizing Tihar festival. The city's art scene is equally impressive, with numerous galleries showcasing traditional and contemporary Nepali art.

When it comes to food, Kathmandu has a lot to offer. From momos (dumplings) to dal bhat (lentil soup with rice), the city's cuisine is a delicious blend of Indian and Tibetan flavors.

One of the most surprising facts about Kathmandu is that it is home to the world's largest concentration of UNESCO World Heritage Sites. The city is also a gateway to the Himalayas, making it a popular destination for trekkers and adventure seekers.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Kathmandu. The city's Durbar Squares, which are home to ancient palaces and temples, are free to visit. The Garden of Dreams, a tranquil oasis in the heart of the city, is also a great place to relax and unwind.

In conclusion, Kathmandu is a city that will capture your heart and leave you wanting more. Its unique blend of culture, traditions, and architecture is a feast for the senses, and its friendly locals will make you feel right at home. Whether you're a history buff, an art lover, or an adventure seeker, Kathmandu has something for everyone.
