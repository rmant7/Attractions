Santiago de Compostela

Santiago de Compostela, the capital of Galicia in Spain, is a city that is steeped in history, culture, and tradition. It is a popular destination for budget travellers who are looking for an authentic Spanish experience without breaking the bank. The city is famous for its stunning architecture, which includes the magnificent Cathedral of Santiago de Compostela, a UNESCO World Heritage Site that attracts millions of visitors every year.

The city is also known for its vibrant festivals, such as the Feast of St. James, which takes place every July and is a celebration of the city's patron saint. The festival is a riot of colour, music, and dance, and is a must-see for anyone visiting Santiago de Compostela.

The city is also home to a thriving arts scene, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the work of local artists. The Galician Centre of Contemporary Art is a particular highlight, with its impressive collection of modern art.

Foodies will love Santiago de Compostela's delicious cuisine, which is based on fresh, locally sourced ingredients. The city is famous for its seafood, with dishes such as octopus and scallops being particular favourites. The local wine, Albariño, is also a must-try for anyone visiting the region.

One of the most surprising facts about Santiago de Compostela is that it is the final destination of the Camino de Santiago, a pilgrimage route that has been walked by millions of people over the centuries. The city is therefore a hub of spiritual and cultural significance, with a unique atmosphere that is hard to find anywhere else.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Santiago de Compostela. The city's parks and gardens are a great place to relax and take in the stunning scenery, while the historic old town is a maze of narrow streets and alleyways that are perfect for exploring on foot.

In conclusion, Santiago de Compostela is a city that has something for everyone. Its rich history, culture, and traditions, combined with its stunning architecture, vibrant festivals, delicious food, and unique atmosphere, make it a must-visit destination for budget travellers who are looking for an authentic Spanish experience.
