Trabzon

Trabzon, a city located on the Black Sea coast of Turkey, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. The city is rich in history and traditions, with a unique blend of Ottoman and Byzantine architecture that will leave you in awe. Trabzon is also known for its vibrant festivals, such as the International Folk Dance Festival and the Black Sea Festival, which showcase the city's rich cultural heritage.

One of the most intriguing aspects of Trabzon is its cuisine, which is heavily influenced by the Black Sea region. The national dish, hamsi pilavı, is a delicious combination of anchovies and rice that will tantalize your taste buds. The city is also famous for its tea, which is grown in the surrounding hills and served in traditional tulip-shaped glasses.

Trabzon is home to many free or low-cost attractions, such as the Hagia Sophia Museum, which was originally built as a church in the 13th century and later converted into a mosque. The Atatürk Mansion, a beautiful 19th-century villa overlooking the Black Sea, is another must-see attraction. For nature lovers, the Uzungöl Lake and the Sümela Monastery, nestled in the mountains, offer breathtaking views and a peaceful retreat from the hustle and bustle of the city.

What sets Trabzon apart from other cities in Turkey is its unique blend of cultures and traditions. The city's proximity to the Black Sea has influenced its cuisine, music, and art, creating a distinct identity that is both fascinating and alluring. Trabzon is a city that will surprise and intrigue you, with its rich history, stunning architecture, and warm hospitality.
