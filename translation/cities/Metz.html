Metz

Metz, a charming city in northeastern France, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. With a rich history dating back to the Roman era, Metz boasts a unique blend of French and German influences, reflected in its stunning architecture and delicious cuisine.

The city's Gothic-style cathedral, Saint-Étienne de Metz, is a must-see attraction, with its towering spires and intricate stained glass windows. The Centre Pompidou-Metz, a contemporary art museum, is another highlight, featuring works by renowned artists such as Picasso and Matisse.

Metz is also known for its lively festivals, including the Fêtes de la Mirabelle, a celebration of the local Mirabelle plum, and the Christmas markets, which transform the city into a winter wonderland.

Foodies will delight in the local cuisine, which includes hearty dishes such as quiche Lorraine and choucroute garnie, a traditional Alsatian dish of sauerkraut and sausages. And for those with a sweet tooth, the city's patisseries offer delectable treats such as macarons and tarte aux mirabelles.

Surprising facts about Metz include its underground aqueducts, which date back to the Roman era, and the fact that the city was once a major center for the production of textiles and tobacco.

Budget travellers will appreciate the many free or low-cost attractions in Metz, such as the Jardin Botanique, a beautiful botanical garden, and the Musée de la Cour d'Or, a museum showcasing the city's history and art.

In contrast to the bustling cities of Paris and Lyon, Metz offers a more relaxed and authentic experience, with its charming streets and friendly locals. So why not add Metz to your travel itinerary and discover this hidden gem for yourself?
