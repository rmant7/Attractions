Malaga

Malaga, the vibrant city on the southern coast of Spain, is a budget traveller's paradise. With its rich culture, stunning architecture, and delicious food, Malaga offers an unforgettable experience without breaking the bank.

The city is steeped in history and tradition, with a fascinating mix of Roman, Moorish, and Christian influences. The stunning Alcazaba fortress, built by the Moors in the 11th century, is a must-see attraction, offering breathtaking views of the city and the sea. The Cathedral of Malaga, with its impressive Baroque façade, is another architectural gem that should not be missed.

Malaga is also known for its lively festivals, including the famous Feria de Agosto, a week-long celebration of music, dance, and food. The city is also home to a thriving art scene, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the works of local and international artists.

Foodies will love Malaga's delicious cuisine, which features fresh seafood, cured meats, and a variety of tapas. Don't miss the chance to try the city's signature dish, espeto de sardinas, grilled sardines on a stick, cooked over an open fire on the beach.

For budget travellers, Malaga offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions, including the beautiful botanical gardens, the Picasso Museum, and the stunning beaches of the Costa del Sol. Take a stroll through the charming historic center, with its narrow streets and colorful buildings, or relax in one of the many parks and plazas.

With its warm climate, stunning scenery, and rich cultural heritage, Malaga is a city that will capture your heart and leave you wanting more. Come and discover this hidden gem for yourself and experience the magic of Andalusia.
