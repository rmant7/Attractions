Reus

Reus, a charming city located in the heart of Catalonia, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic Spanish experience. This city is known for its rich culture and traditions, which are reflected in its stunning architecture, vibrant festivals, and mouth-watering national food.

The city's architecture is a blend of Gothic, Renaissance, and Modernist styles, with notable buildings such as the Gaudí Centre and the Casa Navàs. Reus is also famous for its festivals, including the Festa Major, which takes place in August and features parades, fireworks, and traditional dances.

Art lovers will appreciate the city's many museums, including the Salvador Vilaseca Museum and the Pere Mata Institute, which houses a collection of modern art. Foodies will be delighted by the local cuisine, which includes dishes such as paella, tapas, and the famous Catalan dessert, crema catalana.

Reus is also home to some surprising facts, such as being the birthplace of the famous architect Antoni Gaudí and the inventor of Vermouth, a popular aperitif. Visitors can explore these unique features by visiting the Gaudí Centre and the Vermouth Museum.

For budget travellers, Reus offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions, such as the Municipal Theatre, the Sant Pere Church, and the beautiful Gaudi-designed Pere Mata Institute. The city's parks and gardens, such as the Parc de Sant Jordi and the Parc de la Festa, are also great places to relax and enjoy the scenery.

In conclusion, Reus is a city that offers a unique blend of culture, history, and natural beauty, making it an ideal destination for budget travellers looking for an authentic Spanish experience. With its stunning architecture, vibrant festivals, delicious food, and surprising facts, Reus is a city that will leave a lasting impression on any visitor.
