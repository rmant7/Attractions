Baku

Baku, the capital city of Azerbaijan, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an exotic and culturally rich destination. This vibrant city is a melting pot of ancient traditions and modern architecture, offering a unique blend of old and new. The city's rich history is reflected in its stunning architecture, with the iconic Maiden Tower and the Palace of the Shirvanshahs being must-see attractions.

Baku is also known for its vibrant festivals, including the Novruz Bayrami, a celebration of the Persian New Year, and the Baku International Jazz Festival, which attracts music lovers from around the world. The city's art scene is equally impressive, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the works of local and international artists.

One of the highlights of any visit to Baku is the opportunity to sample the city's delicious national cuisine. From succulent kebabs to mouth-watering plov, the local food is a feast for the senses. Visitors can also indulge in traditional Azerbaijani tea, served in ornate glass cups with sugar cubes and lemon slices.

Baku is full of surprises, with many unique features that are sure to intrigue visitors. For example, the city is home to the world's first oil well, which was drilled in the mid-19th century. Visitors can also explore the ancient petroglyphs at Gobustan National Park, which date back to the Stone Age.

For budget travellers, Baku offers a range of free or low-cost attractions, including the picturesque Old City, the bustling Baku Boulevard, and the stunning Flame Towers. Visitors can also take a stroll along the Caspian Sea promenade or explore the city's many parks and gardens.

In summary, Baku is a city that offers something for everyone, from its rich cultural heritage to its modern amenities and vibrant nightlife. With its unique blend of old and new, Baku is a destination that is sure to captivate and inspire visitors.
