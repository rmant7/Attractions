Bandar Seri Begawan

Bandar Seri Begawan, the capital city of Brunei, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. This city boasts a rich history and a unique blend of Malay, Chinese, and Islamic cultures. The architecture of the city is a sight to behold, with its grand mosques, intricate carvings, and stunning water villages. The city is also home to a number of festivals throughout the year, including the Hari Raya Aidilfitri, which celebrates the end of Ramadan.

The art scene in Bandar Seri Begawan is thriving, with a number of galleries and museums showcasing the work of local artists. The national food of Brunei is a must-try for any foodie, with dishes like Ambuyat, a starchy dish made from sago palm, and Nasi Katok, a simple yet delicious meal of rice, fried chicken, and sambal.

One surprising fact about Bandar Seri Begawan is that it is home to the largest residential palace in the world, the Istana Nurul Iman, which is the official residence of the Sultan of Brunei. Another interesting feature of the city is the Kampong Ayer, a water village that is home to over 30,000 people and is often referred to as the "Venice of the East."

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Bandar Seri Begawan. The Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque, with its golden dome and stunning architecture, is a must-visit. The Taman Haji Sir Muda Omar Ali Saifuddien, a beautiful park located in the heart of the city, is also a great spot for a picnic or a leisurely stroll.

In contrast to other bustling cities, Bandar Seri Begawan offers a peaceful and serene atmosphere, making it the perfect destination for travellers seeking a unique cultural experience. With its rich history, stunning architecture, delicious food, and friendly locals, Bandar Seri Begawan is a city that will leave a lasting impression on any budget traveller.
