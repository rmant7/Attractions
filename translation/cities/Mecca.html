Mecca

Mecca, the holiest city in Islam, is a must-visit destination for budget travellers seeking a spiritual and cultural experience. The city is steeped in history and tradition, with stunning architecture that will leave you in awe. The Kaaba, the most sacred site in Islam, is located in the heart of the city and draws millions of pilgrims every year.

Mecca is a city that never sleeps, with a vibrant atmosphere that is sure to captivate your senses. The city is home to a rich culture and traditions that are reflected in its art, festivals, and national food. The local cuisine is a blend of Middle Eastern and African flavors, with dishes like shawarma, falafel, and hummus that are sure to tantalize your taste buds.

One of the most surprising facts about Mecca is that it is closed to non-Muslims, making it a truly exclusive destination for those who follow the Islamic faith. However, there are still plenty of free or low-cost attractions that budget travellers can enjoy, such as the Jabal al-Nour mountain, which offers stunning views of the city, and the Masjid al-Haram, which is the largest mosque in the world.

The city's architecture is a unique blend of modern and traditional styles, with towering skyscrapers standing alongside ancient mosques and palaces. The contrast between old and new is truly breathtaking and adds to the city's charm.

Mecca is also home to a number of festivals and events throughout the year, including the Hajj pilgrimage, which is one of the largest gatherings of people in the world. The city comes alive during this time, with millions of pilgrims from all over the world coming together to worship and celebrate.

In conclusion, Mecca is a city that is rich in culture, history, and tradition. It is a destination that will leave you with a sense of awe and wonder, and is a must-visit for budget travellers seeking a unique and spiritual experience.
