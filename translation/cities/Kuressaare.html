Kuressaare

Kuressaare, the charming capital of Saaremaa Island in Estonia, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking a unique cultural experience. This picturesque city boasts a rich history and a vibrant local culture that is sure to captivate visitors. The city's stunning architecture, with its medieval castle and quaint streets, is a testament to its past as a trading hub in the Baltic Sea.

Kuressaare is also known for its lively festivals, including the Saaremaa Opera Days and the Kuressaare Maritime Festival, which celebrate the city's maritime heritage. Visitors can also explore the city's art scene, with galleries showcasing local artists and their works.

Foodies will delight in the city's national cuisine, which features fresh seafood and locally sourced ingredients. The traditional dish of Saaremaa, the black bread and herring, is a must-try for any visitor.

For those on a budget, Kuressaare offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions, such as the Kuressaare Castle Park and the Saaremaa Museum. Visitors can also take a stroll along the city's beautiful beaches or explore the nearby forests and nature reserves.

What sets Kuressaare apart from other cities is its unique blend of old-world charm and modern amenities. The city's historic architecture and cobblestone streets are juxtaposed with trendy cafes and boutique shops, creating a one-of-a-kind atmosphere.

Overall, Kuressaare is a must-visit destination for budget travellers looking for an authentic cultural experience. With its rich history, vibrant local culture, and stunning natural beauty, this city is sure to leave a lasting impression on any visitor.
