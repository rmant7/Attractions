Nice

Nice, the capital of the French Riviera, is a city that offers a perfect blend of culture, history, and natural beauty. This charming city is a paradise for budget travellers who want to explore the best of France without breaking the bank. The city is known for its stunning architecture, vibrant festivals, and mouth-watering cuisine.

Nice is a city that is steeped in history and culture. The city's old town, known as Vieux Nice, is a maze of narrow streets and colourful buildings that date back to the 17th century. The city is also home to several museums and art galleries, including the Musée Matisse and the Musée d'Art Moderne et d'Art Contemporain.

One of the most exciting things about Nice is its festivals. The city hosts several festivals throughout the year, including the Nice Carnival, which is one of the largest carnivals in the world. The carnival features colourful parades, music, and dancing, and is a must-see for anyone visiting the city.

Nice is also known for its delicious cuisine. The city is famous for its socca, a savoury pancake made from chickpea flour, and its salade niçoise, a salad made with tuna, olives, and tomatoes. Visitors can also sample local wines and cheeses at the city's many markets and restaurants.

Despite its reputation as a luxury destination, Nice has plenty of free and low-cost attractions. Visitors can stroll along the Promenade des Anglais, a scenic waterfront promenade, or explore the city's many parks and gardens. The city also has several beaches that are free to access.

In conclusion, Nice is a city that has something for everyone. Whether you're interested in history, culture, or food, this charming city is sure to delight. With its stunning architecture, vibrant festivals, and delicious cuisine, Nice is a must-visit destination for budget travellers.
