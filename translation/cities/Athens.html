Athens

Athens, the capital city of Greece, is a budget traveller's paradise. This ancient city is steeped in history, culture, and tradition, making it a must-visit destination for anyone looking to explore the roots of Western civilization. The city's architecture is a blend of ancient and modern, with iconic landmarks like the Acropolis and the Parthenon standing tall amidst the bustling cityscape.

Athens is a city that celebrates its culture and traditions with great fervor. The city is home to numerous festivals and events throughout the year, including the Athens Festival, which showcases the best of Greek theatre, music, and dance. The city's art scene is also thriving, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the works of some of Greece's most talented artists.

One of the highlights of any trip to Athens is the city's national food. Greek cuisine is renowned for its fresh ingredients, bold flavors, and healthy options. From the classic Greek salad to the mouth-watering souvlaki, there is something for everyone to enjoy.

Athens is also full of surprises. Did you know that the city has more than 3,000 years of history? Or that it was the birthplace of democracy? These fascinating facts only add to the allure of this incredible city.

For budget travellers, Athens offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions. The Acropolis Museum, the National Archaeological Museum, and the Ancient Agora are just a few of the must-see sights that won't break the bank.

In Athens, the senses are constantly stimulated. The smell of fresh herbs and spices wafts through the air, while the sound of traditional Greek music fills the streets. The vibrant colors of the city's architecture and the stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea are sure to leave a lasting impression.

Athens is a city like no other, where ancient history and modern culture collide. It's a city that will capture your heart and leave you wanting more.
