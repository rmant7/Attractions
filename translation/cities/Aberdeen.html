Aberdeen

Aberdeen, the Granite City, is a hidden gem in Scotland that is perfect for budget travellers. This city is rich in culture and traditions that are sure to captivate your senses. The architecture of Aberdeen is a sight to behold, with its stunning granite buildings that give the city its nickname. The city is also home to a variety of festivals throughout the year, including the Aberdeen International Youth Festival and the Aberdeen Jazz Festival.

Art lovers will be delighted to know that Aberdeen is home to a number of galleries and museums, including the Aberdeen Art Gallery and the Maritime Museum. The national food of Scotland, haggis, is a must-try for any visitor to Aberdeen. The city also boasts a thriving food scene, with a variety of restaurants and cafes that cater to all tastes and budgets.

One of the most surprising facts about Aberdeen is that it is home to the oldest business in the UK, the Aberdeen Harbour Board, which has been operating since 1136. Another interesting fact is that Aberdeen is the oil capital of Europe, with many oil companies having their headquarters in the city.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Aberdeen. The Duthie Park is a beautiful green space that is perfect for a picnic or a leisurely stroll. The beach at Aberdeen is also a great place to relax and soak up the sun. For those who love history, the Tolbooth Museum is a must-visit, with its fascinating exhibits on the history of crime and punishment in Aberdeen.

In conclusion, Aberdeen is a city that is full of surprises and delights. Its unique culture, traditions, and architecture make it a must-visit destination for any budget traveller. With its free or low-cost attractions, there is something for everyone in this beautiful city.
