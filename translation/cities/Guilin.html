Guilin

Guilin, a city in southern China, is a budget traveller's paradise. This city is known for its stunning natural beauty, with its picturesque mountains, crystal-clear rivers, and lush greenery. Guilin is a city that is steeped in history and culture, with a rich heritage that dates back thousands of years. The city's architecture is a blend of traditional Chinese and modern styles, with ancient temples and pagodas standing alongside modern skyscrapers.

Guilin is also famous for its festivals, which are celebrated throughout the year. The most popular festival is the Lantern Festival, which takes place in February and is a celebration of the end of the Chinese New Year. The festival is marked by the release of thousands of lanterns into the sky, creating a breathtaking spectacle.

The city is also home to a thriving art scene, with many galleries and museums showcasing the work of local artists. The national food of Guilin is rice noodles, which are a staple of the local cuisine. These noodles are served in a variety of dishes, including soups, stir-fries, and salads.

One of the most surprising facts about Guilin is that it is home to the world's largest artificial waterfall, which is located in the city's central square. Another interesting fact is that the city is surrounded by karst mountains, which are unique rock formations that are found only in a few places in the world.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Guilin. The city's parks and gardens are a great place to relax and enjoy the natural beauty of the area. The Seven Star Park is a popular destination, with its caves, waterfalls, and zoo. The Reed Flute Cave is another must-see attraction, with its stunning rock formations and underground lakes.

In conclusion, Guilin is a city that offers something for everyone. Its natural beauty, rich culture, and unique attractions make it a must-visit destination for budget travellers. Whether you're interested in history, art, or simply enjoying the great outdoors, Guilin has something to offer.
