Beijing

Beijing, the capital city of China, is a fascinating destination for budget travellers seeking to immerse themselves in a rich cultural experience. The city is steeped in history and tradition, with a wealth of ancient architecture and landmarks that are sure to captivate visitors. From the iconic Great Wall of China to the Forbidden City, there is no shortage of awe-inspiring sights to explore.

Beijing is also home to a vibrant arts and culture scene, with numerous festivals and events throughout the year celebrating everything from traditional Chinese opera to contemporary art. The city's cuisine is also a highlight, with a diverse range of dishes that reflect the country's rich culinary heritage. From Peking duck to steaming bowls of noodles, there is something to suit every taste.

One of the most surprising facts about Beijing is that it is home to the world's largest public square, Tiananmen Square, which can hold up to one million people. The city is also known for its impressive public transportation system, which makes it easy and affordable to get around.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Beijing. The city's many parks and gardens offer a peaceful respite from the hustle and bustle of the city, while the Temple of Heaven and the Summer Palace are both stunning examples of traditional Chinese architecture.

Overall, Beijing is a city that offers a unique blend of ancient history and modern culture, making it an unforgettable destination for budget travellers looking to experience the best of China.
