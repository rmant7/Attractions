Mahon

Mahon, the capital city of Menorca, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic Spanish experience. This charming city boasts a rich history and culture, evident in its stunning architecture and traditions. The city's most iconic landmark is the impressive Santa Maria Church, a beautiful example of Gothic architecture that dominates the skyline.

Mahon is also famous for its lively festivals, including the annual Sant Joan festival, where locals gather to celebrate with fireworks, music, and traditional Menorcan dancing. The city's art scene is equally impressive, with numerous galleries showcasing the works of local artists.

Foodies will be delighted by the city's delicious national cuisine, which includes mouth-watering dishes such as caldereta de langosta (lobster stew) and ensaimadas (sweet pastries). Visitors can also sample the local gin, which is produced in Mahon and has a unique flavour due to the island's juniper berries.

One of the most surprising facts about Mahon is that it has the second-largest natural harbour in the world, after Pearl Harbor in Hawaii. Visitors can take a boat tour of the harbour to admire the stunning views and learn about the city's maritime history.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Mahon. The city's historic centre is a maze of narrow streets and alleyways, perfect for exploring on foot. The Mercat des Claustre, a bustling market selling fresh produce and local crafts, is also worth a visit.

In contrast to the busy city centre, the nearby Parc Natural de s'Albufera des Grau offers a peaceful retreat for nature lovers. This protected area is home to a variety of bird species and boasts beautiful beaches and hiking trails.

Overall, Mahon is a city that offers something for everyone, from its rich history and culture to its delicious food and stunning natural beauty. It's a must-visit destination for budget travellers looking for an authentic Spanish experience.
