Murcia

Murcia is a hidden gem in the southeast of Spain, perfect for budget travellers seeking an authentic Spanish experience. This vibrant city is steeped in history and culture, with stunning architecture and a rich artistic heritage. The city is known for its beautiful Baroque cathedral, which dominates the skyline and is a must-see for any visitor.

Murcia is also famous for its festivals, including the Holy Week processions and the Feria de Murcia, a week-long celebration of the city's culture and traditions. The city's food scene is also a highlight, with delicious local dishes such as zarangollo, a type of omelette made with courgettes and onions, and paparajotes, a sweet dessert made with lemon leaves.

One of the most surprising facts about Murcia is that it is home to the second-largest orchard in Europe, the Huerta de Murcia, which is a beautiful green oasis in the heart of the city. Visitors can explore the orchard on foot or by bike, and enjoy the fresh produce grown there.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Murcia. The city's museums, including the Salzillo Museum and the Archaeological Museum, offer free entry on certain days of the week. The city's parks and gardens, such as the Floridablanca Garden and the Malecon, are also free to visit and offer a peaceful escape from the hustle and bustle of the city.

Overall, Murcia is a city that offers something for everyone, from its rich cultural heritage to its delicious food and stunning natural beauty. Whether you're a history buff, a foodie, or simply looking for a budget-friendly destination, Murcia is the perfect choice.
