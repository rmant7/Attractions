Colombo

Colombo, the bustling capital city of Sri Lanka, is a vibrant and exciting destination for budget travellers. With its rich culture and traditions, stunning architecture, and delicious national food, Colombo offers a unique and unforgettable experience.

The city is a melting pot of cultures, with influences from India, Portugal, the Netherlands, and Britain. This is reflected in its stunning architecture, from the colonial-era buildings in the Fort area to the colorful Hindu temples and mosques scattered throughout the city.

Colombo is also home to a number of festivals and events throughout the year, including the vibrant Kandy Esala Perahera, a ten-day festival celebrating the Tooth Relic of the Buddha. The city is also known for its vibrant art scene, with galleries and exhibitions showcasing the work of local artists.

One of the highlights of any visit to Colombo is the food. Sri Lankan cuisine is a delicious blend of spices, flavors, and textures, with dishes like hoppers, kottu roti, and string hoppers sure to tantalize your taste buds.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Colombo. Take a stroll through the beautiful Viharamahadevi Park, visit the National Museum to learn about Sri Lanka's rich history, or explore the bustling Pettah Market for a taste of local life.

What might surprise visitors to Colombo is the city's modern side, with sleek skyscrapers and trendy cafes popping up alongside the traditional architecture. This contrast creates a unique and exciting atmosphere that is sure to captivate visitors.

Overall, Colombo is a must-visit destination for budget travellers looking for a unique and unforgettable experience. With its rich culture, stunning architecture, delicious food, and vibrant atmosphere, it is sure to leave a lasting impression.
