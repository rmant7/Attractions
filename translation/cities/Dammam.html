Dammam

Dammam, the capital of the Eastern Province of Saudi Arabia, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic Arabian experience. The city is a melting pot of cultures, traditions, and architecture, with a rich history dating back to ancient times. Dammam is known for its vibrant festivals, including the Al-Janadriyah cultural festival, which showcases the country's heritage through art, music, and dance.

The city's architecture is a blend of modern and traditional styles, with towering skyscrapers and ancient mosques coexisting side by side. The Al Danah district is a must-visit for its stunning traditional houses and narrow alleys that transport visitors back in time.

Dammam's cuisine is a feast for the senses, with a variety of dishes that reflect the city's diverse cultural influences. From the spicy shawarma to the sweet and savory kabsa, the local food is a treat for the taste buds.

One of the surprising facts about Dammam is that it is home to the world's largest oil refinery, which can be visited for free. The city also boasts a beautiful waterfront, the Corniche, where visitors can enjoy a leisurely stroll or a picnic with stunning views of the Arabian Gulf.

For budget travellers, Dammam offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions, such as the King Fahd Park, which features a beautiful lake, playgrounds, and walking trails. The city's museums, including the Dammam Regional Museum, offer a glimpse into the region's history and culture.

In contrast to the bustling city center, the nearby Al Khobar is a peaceful oasis with serene beaches and a laid-back atmosphere. Visitors can take a short drive to explore this charming town and enjoy its many cafes and restaurants.

Overall, Dammam is a city that offers a unique blend of modernity and tradition, with plenty of attractions and experiences that won't break the bank. Whether you're interested in history, culture, or simply soaking up the local atmosphere, Dammam is a destination that won't disappoint.
