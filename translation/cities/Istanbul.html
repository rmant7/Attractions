Istanbul

Istanbul, the city that straddles two continents, is a budget traveller's dream destination. This vibrant city is a melting pot of cultures, traditions, and architecture that dates back to the Byzantine and Ottoman empires. The city's rich history is evident in its stunning landmarks, such as the Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, and the Topkapi Palace.

Istanbul is also a hub for art and culture, with numerous museums, galleries, and festivals that showcase the city's creative spirit. The Istanbul Biennial, the Istanbul International Film Festival, and the Istanbul Jazz Festival are just a few of the events that attract visitors from around the world.

The city's culinary scene is also a highlight, with a range of delicious and affordable options for budget travellers. From street food like simit and kebabs to traditional dishes like baklava and Turkish delight, Istanbul's food is a feast for the senses.

One of the most surprising facts about Istanbul is that it is home to more than 3,000 mosques, each with its unique architecture and history. The city's Grand Bazaar, one of the oldest and largest covered markets in the world, is also a must-visit for budget travellers looking for unique souvenirs and gifts.

For those on a tight budget, Istanbul offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions, such as the Galata Tower, the Basilica Cistern, and the Istanbul Modern Art Museum. The city's parks and waterfronts are also great places to relax and take in the stunning views of the Bosphorus.

In Istanbul, the old and the new coexist in perfect harmony, creating a unique and unforgettable experience for budget travellers. Whether you're exploring the city's ancient landmarks or discovering its vibrant street art scene, Istanbul is a city that will capture your heart and leave you wanting more.
