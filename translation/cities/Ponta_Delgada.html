Ponta Delgada

Ponta Delgada, the capital city of the Azores, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. This charming city boasts a rich history and unique architecture, with its cobblestone streets and colorful buildings. The city's festivals and traditions are deeply rooted in its Portuguese heritage, with the Holy Ghost Festival being a highlight of the year.

Art lovers will appreciate the city's vibrant street art scene, with murals and graffiti adorning many of the buildings. Foodies will be delighted by the local cuisine, which includes fresh seafood and traditional dishes such as cozido das furnas, a stew cooked underground using volcanic heat.

One of the most surprising facts about Ponta Delgada is its proximity to stunning natural wonders, such as the Sete Cidades crater lakes and the Furnas hot springs. These attractions can be explored for free or at a low cost, making them perfect for budget travellers.

Visitors can also take advantage of the city's many free attractions, such as the beautiful Jardim António Borges park and the impressive Forte de São Brás. For those seeking a unique experience, a visit to the Capelas hot springs is a must, where visitors can soak in natural pools surrounded by lush greenery.

Overall, Ponta Delgada is a budget-friendly destination that offers a wealth of cultural experiences and natural beauty. Its unique blend of Portuguese heritage and stunning natural landscapes make it a must-visit destination for any traveller seeking an authentic and affordable adventure.
