Hurghada

Hurghada is a vibrant city located on the Red Sea coast of Egypt, offering a perfect blend of sun, sea, and sand for budget travellers. This city is a hub for water sports enthusiasts, with its crystal-clear waters and coral reefs, making it a paradise for snorkelling and diving. The city is also known for its lively nightlife, with a plethora of bars, clubs, and restaurants to choose from.

Hurghada is a melting pot of cultures, with influences from ancient Egyptian, Greek, and Roman civilizations. The city's architecture is a mix of modern and traditional styles, with colourful buildings and mosques dotting the landscape. The city is also home to several festivals throughout the year, including the Hurghada International Festival, which celebrates the city's diverse cultural heritage.

The art scene in Hurghada is thriving, with several galleries showcasing local artists' works. The city's national food is a must-try for budget travellers, with its delicious blend of Middle Eastern and Mediterranean flavours. The local cuisine includes dishes like koshari, a mix of rice, lentils, and pasta, and ful medames, a hearty breakfast dish made with fava beans.

Hurghada is also home to several surprising facts, such as being the second-largest city on the Red Sea coast and having a population of over 250,000 people. The city is also a popular destination for kiteboarding and windsurfing, with its consistent winds and flat waters.

For budget travellers, Hurghada offers several free or low-cost attractions, such as the Hurghada Marina, where visitors can stroll along the waterfront and enjoy the stunning views. The city's beaches are also a great option for those looking to soak up the sun without breaking the bank.

In conclusion, Hurghada is a city that offers something for everyone, from its rich cultural heritage to its stunning natural beauty. With its affordable prices and endless attractions, it's no wonder that this city is a popular destination for budget travellers.
