Oviedo

Oviedo, the capital city of Asturias, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic Spanish experience. This charming city is steeped in history and culture, with a rich tradition of art, architecture, and gastronomy. The city's old town is a maze of narrow streets and alleys, lined with colorful buildings and charming cafes. The stunning Gothic Cathedral of San Salvador dominates the skyline, while the nearby Campoamor Theatre is a must-visit for theatre lovers.

Oviedo is also famous for its unique pre-Romanesque architecture, with several stunning examples scattered throughout the city. The most famous of these is the Santa Maria del Naranco, a UNESCO World Heritage Site that dates back to the 9th century. The city is also home to several museums, including the Museum of Fine Arts and the Archaeological Museum, which offer free admission on certain days.

One of the best times to visit Oviedo is during the city's annual festivals, such as the Feast of San Mateo in September. This lively celebration features traditional music, dance, and food, and is a great way to experience the local culture. Speaking of food, Oviedo is known for its hearty cuisine, with dishes like fabada asturiana (a bean stew) and cachopo (a stuffed beef dish) being local favorites.

For those looking to explore the great outdoors, Oviedo is surrounded by stunning natural beauty, including the Picos de Europa National Park. Hiking, cycling, and kayaking are all popular activities in the area, and there are plenty of low-cost options for those on a budget.

Overall, Oviedo is a city that offers something for everyone, from history buffs to foodies to nature lovers. Its unique blend of culture, tradition, and natural beauty make it a must-visit destination for budget travellers looking to experience the real Spain.
