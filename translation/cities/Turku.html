Turku

Turku, the oldest city in Finland, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. With its rich history, stunning architecture, and vibrant festivals, Turku offers a unique blend of old-world charm and modern sophistication.

The city is steeped in tradition, with a strong focus on preserving its cultural heritage. Visitors can explore the medieval Turku Castle, which dates back to the 13th century, or wander through the cobblestone streets of the Old Town, admiring the colorful wooden houses and quaint cafes.

Turku is also a hub for art and culture, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the works of local artists. The Turku Art Museum, for example, features a diverse collection of contemporary art, while the Aboa Vetus & Ars Nova museum offers a fascinating glimpse into the city's history.

Foodies will delight in Turku's culinary scene, which is centered around traditional Finnish cuisine. From hearty stews and soups to fresh seafood and berries, there's something to satisfy every palate. Don't miss the chance to try the local specialty, Turun sinappi, a sweet and spicy mustard that's a staple in Finnish kitchens.

For those on a budget, Turku offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions. Take a stroll along the Aura River, which runs through the heart of the city, or visit the Turku Market Square, where vendors sell fresh produce and handmade crafts. The Turku Cathedral, with its stunning Gothic architecture, is also a must-see.

One of the most surprising facts about Turku is that it was once the capital of Finland, before Helsinki took over the title in 1812. Despite this, Turku remains an important cultural and economic center, with a thriving arts scene and a bustling port.

In contrast to Helsinki's cosmopolitan vibe, Turku offers a more laid-back and intimate atmosphere, making it the perfect destination for budget travellers seeking a unique and authentic experience. So why not pack your bags and discover the magic of Turku for yourself?
