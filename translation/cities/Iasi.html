Iasi

Iasi, the cultural capital of Romania, is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by budget travellers. This city is a melting pot of cultures, traditions, and architecture that will leave you in awe. The city's rich history is reflected in its stunning buildings, museums, and art galleries. Iasi is also known for its vibrant festivals, such as the International Theatre Festival and the Medieval Festival, which attract visitors from all over the world.

The city's architecture is a mix of Gothic, Baroque, and Neoclassical styles, which can be seen in the impressive Palace of Culture, the iconic Three Hierarchs Church, and the beautiful Golia Monastery. The city's streets are lined with colourful buildings, charming cafes, and bustling markets, making it a perfect place to explore on foot.

Iasi is also a food lover's paradise, with its delicious national dishes such as sarmale (stuffed cabbage rolls), mici (grilled minced meat), and papanasi (sweet cheese dumplings). The city's vibrant food scene is a reflection of its diverse cultural influences.

One of the surprising facts about Iasi is that it is home to the oldest university in Romania, the Alexandru Ioan Cuza University, which was founded in 1860. The city is also known for its impressive botanical gardens, which are home to over 10,000 species of plants.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Iasi, such as the beautiful Copou Park, the stunning Botanical Garden, and the impressive National Museum of Romanian Literature. The city also has a vibrant nightlife scene, with plenty of bars and clubs to choose from.

In conclusion, Iasi is a city that will capture your heart with its rich culture, stunning architecture, delicious food, and vibrant festivals. It is a perfect destination for budget travellers who want to experience the best of Romania without breaking the bank.
