Larnaca

Larnaca, a city located on the southern coast of Cyprus, is a budget traveller's paradise. This charming city is steeped in history and culture, making it an ideal destination for those looking to explore the island's rich heritage without breaking the bank.

Larnaca is home to a plethora of architectural wonders, including the stunning St. Lazarus Church, which dates back to the 9th century. The city's old town is a maze of narrow streets and alleys, lined with traditional Cypriot houses and quaint cafes.

The city is also known for its vibrant festivals, including the annual Kataklysmos festival, which celebrates the arrival of summer with music, dancing, and traditional food. Speaking of food, Larnaca is a foodie's dream, with a wide range of delicious local dishes to try, including souvlaki, moussaka, and halloumi cheese.

One of the most surprising facts about Larnaca is that it is home to the Hala Sultan Tekke, one of the most important Muslim shrines in the world. This stunning mosque is located on the shores of the Salt Lake, which is also a popular spot for birdwatching.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to enjoy in Larnaca. The city's beaches are some of the best in Cyprus, and there are plenty of parks and gardens to explore. The Larnaca Salt Lake is also a must-visit, especially during the winter months when it is home to thousands of flamingos.

In conclusion, Larnaca is a city that offers something for everyone, from its rich history and culture to its stunning architecture and delicious food. Whether you're a budget traveller or simply looking for a unique and exciting destination, Larnaca is sure to leave a lasting impression.
