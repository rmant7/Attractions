Tartu

Tartu, the second-largest city in Estonia, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. This charming city is known for its rich history, stunning architecture, and vibrant arts scene. Tartu is home to the oldest and most prestigious university in Estonia, which has shaped the city's intellectual and creative atmosphere.

The city's architecture is a mix of medieval and modern styles, with the Old Town boasting cobblestone streets and colorful buildings. The city's most iconic landmark is the Tartu Cathedral, a stunning Gothic structure that dates back to the 13th century. Visitors can also explore the ruins of the Tartu Castle, which was destroyed during the Livonian War.

Tartu is a city that celebrates its culture and traditions, with numerous festivals and events throughout the year. The Tartu Hanseatic Days is a popular festival that showcases the city's medieval heritage, while the Tartu Jazz Festival attracts music lovers from around the world.

Art enthusiasts will be delighted by the city's many galleries and museums, including the Tartu Art Museum and the Estonian National Museum. The city is also known for its vibrant street art scene, with colorful murals adorning many of the city's buildings.

No visit to Tartu is complete without sampling the local cuisine. Estonian cuisine is a fusion of Scandinavian, Russian, and German influences, with hearty dishes like black bread, smoked fish, and sauerkraut. Visitors can also try traditional Estonian drinks like Kali, a fermented bread drink, and Vana Tallinn, a sweet liqueur.

For budget travellers, Tartu offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions, including the Botanical Gardens, the Toomemägi Park, and the Tartu Market Square. Visitors can also take a stroll along the Emajõgi River, which runs through the heart of the city.

In conclusion, Tartu is a city that offers something for everyone, from its rich history and stunning architecture to its vibrant arts scene and delicious cuisine. With its affordable prices and welcoming atmosphere, Tartu is the perfect destination for budget travellers looking for an authentic cultural experience.
