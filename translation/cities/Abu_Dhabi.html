Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi, the capital city of the United Arab Emirates, is a fascinating destination for budget travellers seeking a unique cultural experience. The city is a melting pot of traditional Arabian culture and modern architecture, offering a perfect blend of old and new. The city's rich history and heritage are reflected in its stunning architecture, including the iconic Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, which is a must-visit for any traveller.

Abu Dhabi is a city that celebrates its traditions and culture, with a range of festivals and events throughout the year. The annual Abu Dhabi Festival is a highlight, showcasing the best of local and international art and culture. Visitors can also experience the traditional Emirati way of life at the Heritage Village, which offers a glimpse into the city's past.

The city's cuisine is a fusion of Arabic, Indian, and Persian influences, with a range of delicious dishes to try. From the famous shawarma to the traditional Emirati dish of machboos, there is something for every taste bud. Visitors can also indulge in the city's famous dates, which are a staple of the Emirati diet.

Abu Dhabi is also home to a thriving arts scene, with a range of galleries and museums showcasing local and international talent. The Louvre Abu Dhabi is a must-visit, with its stunning architecture and impressive collection of art.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions to explore in Abu Dhabi. The Corniche is a beautiful waterfront promenade that offers stunning views of the city skyline, while the Al Ain Oasis is a peaceful oasis in the heart of the city.

Overall, Abu Dhabi is a city that offers a unique cultural experience for budget travellers. With its rich history, stunning architecture, delicious cuisine, and thriving arts scene, there is something for everyone to enjoy.
