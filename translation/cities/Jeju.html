Jeju

Jeju, the largest island in South Korea, is a budget traveller's paradise. This stunning destination is known for its natural beauty, rich culture, and unique traditions. The island is home to a plethora of attractions that are free or low-cost, making it an ideal destination for those on a budget.

Jeju is a place where ancient traditions and modern culture coexist in perfect harmony. The island is famous for its unique architecture, which is a blend of traditional Korean and Chinese styles. The Jeju Folk Village Museum is a must-visit attraction for those interested in learning about the island's history and culture.

The island is also home to a number of festivals throughout the year, including the Jeju Fire Festival and the Jeju International Wind Festival. These events are a great way to experience the local culture and traditions.

Jeju is also known for its art scene, with many local artists showcasing their work in galleries and exhibitions. The island's national food, Jeju black pork, is a must-try for foodies. This succulent meat is cooked over an open flame and served with a variety of side dishes.

One of the most surprising facts about Jeju is that it is home to the world's largest lava tube system. Visitors can explore these underground caves and marvel at the natural wonders that lie beneath the surface.

For those looking for free or low-cost attractions, Jeju has plenty to offer. The island is home to a number of beautiful beaches, including Hyeopjae Beach and Jungmun Beach. Visitors can also hike up Hallasan, the highest mountain in South Korea, for stunning views of the island.

In contrast to the hustle and bustle of Seoul, Jeju offers a peaceful and serene atmosphere that is sure to appeal to the senses and emotions. With its unique culture, stunning natural beauty, and affordable attractions, Jeju is a must-visit destination for budget travellers.
