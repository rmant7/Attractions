Terceira

Terceira, the third-largest island in the Azores archipelago, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. This island boasts a rich history and unique traditions that are still alive today. The architecture of Terceira is a blend of Portuguese and Azorean styles, with colourful houses and cobblestone streets that transport visitors back in time.

One of the most exciting aspects of Terceira is its vibrant festival culture. The island hosts several festivals throughout the year, including the Sanjoaninas festival in June, which is a celebration of the island's patron saint. During this festival, the streets come alive with music, dancing, and traditional food.

Art lovers will appreciate the island's unique handicrafts, such as the famous Terceira embroidery, which is known for its intricate designs and vibrant colours. Foodies will also be delighted by the island's national dish, Alcatra, a slow-cooked beef stew that is a must-try for any visitor.

Terceira is also home to several natural wonders, including the Algar do Carvão, a volcanic chimney that visitors can explore for free. The island's stunning coastline is perfect for hiking and swimming, and there are several free beaches to enjoy.

What sets Terceira apart from other destinations is its strong sense of community and tradition. Visitors will feel welcomed by the friendly locals and can immerse themselves in the island's unique culture. With its affordable accommodations and low-cost attractions, Terceira is the perfect destination for budget travellers looking for an authentic and unforgettable experience.
