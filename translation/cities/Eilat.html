Eilat

Eilat, the southernmost city of Israel, is a budget traveller's paradise. This vibrant city is nestled between the Red Sea and the Negev Desert, offering a unique blend of natural beauty and modern amenities. Eilat is a melting pot of cultures, with influences from Israel, Egypt, Jordan, and Saudi Arabia. The city's architecture is a mix of modern high-rises and traditional Middle Eastern buildings, creating a unique skyline.

Eilat is known for its vibrant festivals and events, including the Red Sea Jazz Festival and the Eilat Chamber Music Festival. The city is also home to a thriving art scene, with galleries showcasing local and international artists. The national food of Israel, falafel, is a must-try in Eilat, and the city is also known for its fresh seafood.

One of the most surprising facts about Eilat is that it is home to the world's northernmost coral reef. The reef is a popular spot for snorkelling and diving, and visitors can see a variety of colourful fish and other marine life. Another unique feature of Eilat is the Timna Park, a stunning desert landscape with ancient copper mines and rock formations.

For budget travellers, Eilat offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions. The city's beaches are free to access, and visitors can also explore the Eilat Museum and the Botanical Garden for a small fee. The Dolphin Reef is a popular attraction where visitors can swim with dolphins for a reasonable price.

In contrast to other cities in Israel, Eilat has a laid-back and relaxed atmosphere, making it the perfect destination for those looking to unwind and soak up the sun. The city's warm climate and stunning natural beauty appeal to the senses and emotions, creating a truly unforgettable experience for budget travellers.
