Liege

Liege, a charming city in Belgium, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic European experience. This city is a melting pot of cultures, traditions, and architecture, making it a unique destination for those who want to explore beyond the typical tourist spots. Liege is known for its lively festivals, such as the famous "Fiesta de Wallonie," which celebrates the region's culture and heritage.

The city's architecture is a blend of old and new, with stunning Gothic cathedrals and modern buildings coexisting in harmony. The streets are lined with quaint cafes and restaurants, serving up delicious Belgian cuisine, including the famous Liege waffles and beer. The city's art scene is also thriving, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing local and international artists.

Liege is a city of surprises, with hidden gems waiting to be discovered around every corner. One such surprise is the Montagne de Bueren, a staircase with 374 steps that leads to a breathtaking view of the city. Another is the Coteaux de la Citadelle, a beautiful park with stunning views of the Meuse River.

For budget travellers, Liege offers plenty of free or low-cost attractions, such as the Museum of Walloon Life, which showcases the region's history and culture. The city also has numerous parks and gardens, perfect for a picnic or a leisurely stroll. The Sunday market at La Batte is a must-visit, offering a wide range of local produce and goods.

In conclusion, Liege is a city that appeals to all the senses, with its rich culture, stunning architecture, delicious food, and vibrant festivals. It is a city that surprises and intrigues, with hidden gems waiting to be discovered. For budget travellers, Liege offers a unique and authentic European experience without breaking the bank.
