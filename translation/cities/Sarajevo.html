Sarajevo

Sarajevo, the capital city of Bosnia and Herzegovina, is a hidden gem for budget travellers seeking an authentic cultural experience. This city is a melting pot of different cultures, religions, and traditions, which is reflected in its unique architecture, festivals, and art. The city's Ottoman-era bazaar, Baščaršija, is a must-visit for anyone interested in history and architecture. The narrow streets are lined with shops selling traditional Bosnian crafts, such as copperware and handmade carpets.

Sarajevo is also known for its vibrant arts scene, with numerous galleries and museums showcasing the works of local artists. The city's National Museum is a great place to learn about the country's rich history and culture. For foodies, Sarajevo is a paradise. The national dish, cevapi, is a must-try. These grilled meat sausages are served with onions, sour cream, and a type of flatbread called somun.

One of the most surprising facts about Sarajevo is that it hosted the Winter Olympics in 1984. The city's Olympic Stadium is still standing and is open to visitors. Another unique feature of Sarajevo is its mix of religions. The city is home to mosques, churches, and synagogues, all within walking distance of each other.

For budget travellers, there are plenty of free or low-cost attractions in Sarajevo. The city's parks, such as Vrelo Bosne and Trebević, offer stunning views of the surrounding mountains and are perfect for a picnic. The Latin Bridge, where Archduke Franz Ferdinand was assassinated, is a historical landmark that can be visited for free.

In conclusion, Sarajevo is a city that will surprise and intrigue any budget traveller. Its unique culture, traditions, and architecture, combined with its vibrant arts scene and delicious food, make it a must-visit destination. With plenty of free or low-cost attractions, there's no reason not to add Sarajevo to your travel bucket list.
