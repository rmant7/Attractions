Corfu

Corfu, the stunning Greek island in the Ionian Sea, is a budget traveller's paradise. With its rich history, vibrant culture, and breathtaking natural beauty, Corfu offers an unforgettable experience that won't break the bank.

The island is steeped in history and tradition, with a fascinating mix of Greek, Roman, Byzantine, and Venetian influences evident in its architecture, art, and cuisine. The Old Town of Corfu is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, with its narrow streets, ancient fortresses, and stunning Venetian-style buildings.

Corfu is also famous for its festivals, including the Easter celebrations, which are some of the most elaborate and colorful in Greece. The island's music and dance traditions are also a highlight, with lively performances taking place throughout the year.

Foodies will love Corfu's delicious cuisine, which features fresh seafood, locally grown produce, and traditional dishes like pastitsada and sofrito. And for those looking for a unique culinary experience, the island's kumquat liqueur is a must-try.

But Corfu isn't just about culture and cuisine. The island is also home to some of the most beautiful beaches in Greece, with crystal-clear waters and stunning scenery. And for those looking for free or low-cost attractions, there are plenty of options, including hiking trails, historic sites, and local markets.

One of the most surprising facts about Corfu is that it was once home to the legendary Greek hero Odysseus, as mentioned in Homer's epic poem, The Odyssey. And for those looking for a contrast to the island's historic charm, the lively nightlife scene in Corfu Town is sure to impress.

In short, Corfu is a budget traveller's dream destination, offering a unique blend of history, culture, and natural beauty that is sure to leave a lasting impression.
